# GSPS — 30-Second Promo Video Script

**Purpose:** Top-of-funnel video for people who don't have an account yet.
Goal: make GSPS look simple, make "the market can work for you" feel
believable and not scary, and close with a hard CTA to sign up.

**Length:** 30 seconds, 8 shots. Paced for vertical (9:16) first — reels/shorts/TikTok
— with a 16:9 crop-safe center for other placements.

**Tone:** Confident, clean, fast. No jargon in voiceover. On-screen text carries
the technical credibility; the voice carries the emotion.

## Voiceover script (read straight through, ~28–30s at a natural pace)

> "GSPS scans the market and scores every setup — automatically. Every trade
> comes with an entry, a stop, and a target — mapped out before you ever place
> it. No guesswork on size, no second-guessing the plan — one recommendation,
> one tap. Practice with real market prices, in a paper account — so you learn
> the moves before you risk a dollar. This is trading built for people just
> getting started — not Wall Street. GSPS. Sign up free today."

Word count: ~85 words → comfortably fits 28 seconds at a brisk, confident
delivery pace (~180 wpm), leaving room for the silent hook (shot 1) and a beat
on the CTA card (shot 8).

## Production notes

- **Shot 1 has no voiceover** — let the hook line and the product UI breathe
  for a beat before the narration starts. This is the pattern-interrupt that
  stops a scroll.
- **Use real product screens**, not illustrations. Guided Mode's actual card
  UI, the actual scan list, the actual portfolio P&L view. Authenticity reads
  as "this is a real working thing," which matters more for conversion than
  polish.
- **Never show a specific dollar P&L figure** in the portfolio shot that looks
  like a promised return — use small, plausible paper-account numbers, and
  keep the "paper account" / practice framing on screen at the same time so
  the claim can't be read as a real-money result. GSPS is not a financial
  advisor and the video must not imply guaranteed profit — that's a
  compliance line, not a style note.
- **Captions burned in** — most social viewing is sound-off. On-screen text
  in the table above should appear as bold, high-contrast captions timed to
  the shot, not just the voiceover transcript.
- **Music**: upbeat, modern, no lyrics that compete with the voiceover. Cut
  cleanly on the beat at shot changes (2→3, 4→5) if the edit allows.
- **CTA card (shot 8)** should stay on screen a full 2 seconds — long enough
  to screenshot the link. If a QR code is used, test it at reels-compressed
  resolution before publishing; a QR that doesn't scan on a re-compressed
  video is a dead CTA.
- **Legal disclosure**: a one-line disclosure ("Trading involves risk. Paper
  trading shown.") should appear as small burned-in text during shots 5–8,
  matching the footer disclosure already used on the landing page
  (`app/page.tsx`) — consistent messaging, not a new claim to review.
