## 4. Issues to resolve before any build

1. Transcript ends at 'confirm to initialize Mountain 2', which pushes toward entity filing. The gate requires an externally supported business-model validation first; a $1 activation proves payment only.
2. The diagnostic treats projective answers as capability. Label it 'proposed projective diagnostic'. Do not describe as validated psychometrics.
3. The concept (B2B agency, 2 retainers) is unrelated to the operator's actual domain unless the owner confirms it. The engine proposed it from imagery, not market evidence.
4. S scale: S is per-mountain, per-competency or global? V range 8 to 0? What is S=9/V=0? Which support always remains (accessibility, safety, account support, human oversight)?
5. Regression, stale-evidence and dispute process are unspecified.

## 5. Minimal data model (proposal, not implemented)

`isee_gate_records`: entity/operator/project, gate/criterion, artifact or provider ID, timestamp, verification method, evidence source, verifier, freshness/expiry, status, exception, audit history. Tamper-evident and versioned; do not call it immutable. Keep EINs, bank data and filings out of public repos.
