## What this run measures that no earlier run did

This is the first committed run on the replay that arms like production. Everything in the
2026-09-23 and 2026-09-25 runs measured the superseded harness (see their NOTES).

- **The live scan's trigger.** Gann's swing crossing on daily bars, in the macro-derived
  direction, with no STRAT gate. Measured since PR #290.
- **Continuation setups**, gated as `runMarketScan` gates them, and the scan's own 30-bar daily
  minimum.
- **The Gann-only verdict.** No SMA/VWAP anywhere in the scored direction, the macro reads or the
  coarse gates (F2.5).
- **Production's bracket.** Stop, risk and target are fixed from the trigger, as
  `deriveOrderInputFromPlan` attaches them. R is measured on the plan's risk. Fills at or beyond
  the stop or target are dropped and counted (`refusedFills`), because production refuses them
  (`fill_outran_bracket`).
- **Both production entry rules.** `stop` is the resting stop at the trigger: the ticket, Guided
  Mode and the demo account. `confirmed` is the full touch → break (3-point-rule buffer) → retest
  → hold sequence, entered at the next open: plan-scoped automation and the portfolio manager.

**Not yet modelled**, from the handoff's Phase 1:
- F1.2: the target is `trigger + 2R`, not the plan's structural TP1.
- F1.5: no signal-fingerprint de-duplication.
- F1.4: confirmation is advanced every 15-minute bar, not once per scan pass, so the confirmation
  rate here is an upper bound.

## What it supports, and what it doesn't

1. **Entry rule (F3.4): the evidence favours confirmation, on every surface.**
   - `confirmed` beats the stop entry on all trades with non-overlapping CIs.
   - It is positive in both halves, and it is the only cell whose all-trades CI clears 0 under
     the production stop.
   - The handoff's pre-registered Phase 4 rule says to keep the confirmation rule platform-wide
     if it isn't significantly worse than `prodstop`. It is significantly better.
   - That reverses the 2026-09-26 F3.4 decision to leave human-placed orders on the stop entry
     (AGENTS.md records the reversal).
   - Aligning the ticket, Guided and the demo on confirmation is a production and UX change, and
     is held for the owner.
2. **The stop cap costs money under the stop entry.**
   - `prodstop` is worse than `raw` by −0.215R overall and −0.460R on Execute, with separated CIs.
   - `prodstop`'s Execute is negative in both halves. `raw`'s own halves are not stable
     (−0.109 / +0.148).
   - The handoff's pre-registered bar for proposing a cap change also requires the production TP1
     target (F1.2), which isn't modelled. So this is strong evidence, **not** yet a proposal.
   - Under `confirmed`, `stopRoom` flips to −0.23R. The stop question and the entry question
     interact, so re-test the cap on the `confirmed` rule once F1.2 lands.
3. **The 6/3.5 cutoffs can't be re-derived from this run, and the score doesn't separate
   outcomes yet.**
   - In no cell is Execute's CI clear of 0.
   - Execute is not better than Watch in any cell, and under `prodstop` it is the worst bucket.
   - The reports carry buckets, not a per-score-band sweep, so the band-by-band zero-crossing the
     2026-09-23 confirmation relied on can't be read from these files.
   - Per the handoff's Phase 4 rule this points at translation (Phase 3) or the thresholds, not
     the entry model.
   - The cutoffs stay at 6/3.5, unconfirmed, until a run that includes a score-band sweep
     (and F1.2).
