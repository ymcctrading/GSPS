## What the runs say

1. **Execute now beats Watch on every bracket cell.** Since B1 (Gann's own swing charts) the
   Execute bucket is positive under both entry rules, and above Watch in all four bracket
   cells. That is the first time on a production-faithful replay. The 6/3.5 cutoffs hold on
   this evidence. There is no per-score-band split in the report to move them with, and the
   Execute samples (38–60) are too small to justify moving them either way.
2. **The confirmed entry still wins.** +0.190R overall in both runs against −0.052/−0.070 for
   the stop entry. This is the evidence behind C5 (confirmation on every path, automation
   included).
3. **Run 11's Execute bucket is smaller and weaker than run 8's** (60 → 38/44 trades,
   +0.434 → +0.350 confirmed). Run 11 changed several things at once (the Stage A/B2 trend,
   time and level infusion, and the breakaway hold), so the drop can't be pinned on the
   breakaway rule alone. A later run should isolate it.
4. **Gann's exits: better across the population, worse on Execute, and not yet a like-for-like
   result.**
   - With no fixed target, the gain comes almost entirely from trades still open at the
     replay's 10-session limit (`timeout`: +3.9R and +5.7R average, 59 and 80 trades). Those
     are marked to market, not closed by any rule, so they overstate what a finished trade
     would realise.
   - Stops at the initial level lose −1.1 to −1.2R, break-even exits land near 0, the hold
     test cuts losers (−0.7 to −1.5R), and the final-stage trail is small but strongly
     positive (n 2 and 9).
   - On Execute, Gann exits did worse than the bracket (−0.011 vs +0.350 confirmed; +0.099 vs
     +0.220 stop).
   - The report's win rate and profit factor are meaningless for the Gann cells: they count
     only `win`/`loss` outcomes, and most Gann-exit trades end as `timeout` or `break_even`.
     Read expectancy only.

## Decision taken from this

The live exit manager stays on the bracket for now. The switch was conditional on the replay
agreeing, and it doesn't yet: the Execute bucket, the one Guided Mode and automation actually
trade, is worse under Gann's exits, and the population-wide gain rests on marked-to-market
open trades. The next measurement needs a longer holding horizon (so trades close on a rule
rather than a timeout) before this is decided.

## 2026-09-28: why the six-year results were bad (run 16, and the fixes it led to)

Run 16 ([36386238505](https://github.com/ymcctrading/GSPS/actions/runs/36386238505)) replayed
the same 766 symbols on 15-minute bars (2026-07-30 → 2026-09-25) at `2026-09-28-gann-exits-figures`,
one cell per suspected cause. Files: `2026-09-28-15Min-2R-within-all-766sym-diag-*.json`.

| Cell | All trades | Execute | Watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| legacy-confirmed (the replay's old ATR stop, 2R target) | +0.193 (352) [+0.048, +0.343] | +0.306 (44) | +0.172 (252) |
| plan-confirmed (production's own plan stop and TP1) | +0.100 (384) [+0.034, +0.166]; halves −0.004 / +0.204 | +0.198 (51) [−0.007, +0.397] | +0.091 (271) |
| plan-confirmed-gannrunner (what went live) | +0.023 (384) [−0.029, +0.076] | −0.014 (51) | +0.029 (271) |
| … + pyramid | identical to the row above (see finding 3) | | |
| … + round-number hold | +0.032 (333) | −0.013 (45) | +0.037 (232) |

### Findings

1. **The replay was not trading production's plan.** It capped the stop at 2.5–3.5× the
   execution-bar ATR with a 2R target; production uses the equity plan (a structural stop,
   level-based TP1). Fixed with `usePlanLevels` (committed earlier the same day). On the plan's
   own levels the run is still positive, and Execute still beats Watch.
2. **The main cause of the losses: a mistranslated Gann allowance in the exit rules.** Gann's
   hold test says a market that crossed an old top "should not react back 3 points under it".
   He scales points with price himself: 3 points on $25–60 stocks, 5–10 on $100–300. The code
   used the entry's 0.3% lost-motion margin, about a tenth of that. So the hold test fired on
   ordinary noise:
   - Here it ended 224 of 384 trades at −0.226R each (−50.6R in total), against +1.1R for
     final-stage exits and +60.7R for trades still open at the time limit.
   - On the six-year one-hour run (run 12) it was the costliest rule as well (−0.51R, n 3,367).
   - The same too-tight allowance also set the trailing stops under swing bottoms and the
     prior month's low.

   Fixed in `lib/gann/pointScale.ts`. The allowance is a smooth curve through the middle of
   Gann's two stated bands: 7.1% at $42.50, 3.75% at $200, clamped to 1–12%. It is read by
   `lib/gann/exitRules.ts` on the live exit manager and the replay alike. The entry still
   crosses by the lost-motion margin, which is a separate rule.
3. **Pyramiding never fired in the replay.** Lots are fractions there, and `readPyramidAdd` rounded
   half of 1 share to zero. That is fixed. The harness's identical-trades warning is what caught it.
4. **The round-number hold** (no automated order at a figure unless the plan clears it) changes
   little: +0.032 vs +0.023, with 51 fewer trades.
5. **The two non-Gann gaps were closed on the owner's instruction:**
   - Publication no longer needs a STRAT pattern. The continuation pass now requires Gann's
     with-the-daily-trend crossing (`isGannContinuation`) on the scan and the replay alike.
   - The intraday alerts read the session's 50% point instead of VWAP.
6. `STRATEGY_VERSION` is `2026-09-28-gann-points-continuation`. The final runs are on it (next
   section).

## 2026-09-28: the F4 calendar test (research only)

Run [36386507801](https://github.com/ymcctrading/GSPS/actions/runs/36386507801), pre-registered in
`docs/memory-bank/F4_CYCLES_CALENDAR_RESEARCH.md` Part 3. Turns are completed pivots of Gann's
weekly swing chart on daily bars. The base rate is every circular shift of the window set within
the year, with Holm–Bonferroni across the 12 cells.

**Deviation from the plan:**
- **The data window.** The plan fixed 1990 onward. The only feed reachable here (Alpaca's free
  IEX daily bars) returned SPY from 2018-11 and DIA from 2020-07, so 2019–2025 for SPY
  (56 window instances per cell) and 2021–2025 for DIA (40).
- **The power.** A test this short cannot detect a modest effect.

| Set | Convention | SPY hit rate vs base | p | DIA hit rate vs base | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 (permanent cycle, 1930) | calendar | 0.375 vs 0.451 | 0.95 | 0.375 vs 0.394 | 0.68 |
| W1 | solar (from the equinox) | 0.357 vs 0.452 | 0.97 | 0.375 vs 0.395 | 0.68 |
| W1 | 364-day year | 0.500 vs 0.456 | 0.28 | 0.475 vs 0.397 | 0.20 |
| W3 (1940 forecast windows) | calendar | 0.661 vs 0.629 | 0.35 | 0.525 vs 0.600 | 0.91 |
| W3 | solar | 0.661 vs 0.629 | 0.35 | 0.525 vs 0.600 | 0.91 |
| W3 | 364-day year | 0.643 vs 0.629 | 0.46 | 0.600 vs 0.601 | 0.61 |

**Finding:**
- No cell beats random placement of the same windows, before or after the Holm correction.
- W1 on the calendar and solar years sits *below* its base rate on both series.
- The 364-day year is the only convention above base on both, but not significantly.
- On this record, Gann's dated windows do not catch weekly-chart turns more often than chance.

**What that means for the platform:** they stay context only (they never gated anything), and
nothing about them may be presented as predictive. The phase blocks in that run were empty,
because each block dropped its own first and last years. That is fixed (`scoreCell`'s `years`),
and the blocks are re-run with the next calendar pass. A pre-1990 or pre-2018 record would need
another data source; none is reachable from this environment.
