## "Production stop" is a cap, not a widening

The rendered files label `prodstop` as "production (leeway + large-cap widening)". In effect,
`computeStopWithLeeway` (`lib/strat/levels.ts`) does something else:

1. It pushes the stop out by a small ATR leeway.
2. It then **clamps it to at most 2.5× the 15-minute ATR** (3.5× for large caps).

In `raw`, 1,390 of 1,444 Execute stops sat at 2.5× ATR or more. Under `prodstop`:

- 639 of them are pulled in to the 2–2.5× band, which is the 2.5× cap applied to non-large-caps.
  Only 2,207 of all 29,126 trades were classed as large-cap.
- 779 remain at 2.5× or more, within the 3.5× large-cap cap.

So for much of the Execute bucket the production stop is *tighter*, not wider. Mean Execute holding
time falls from 76.6 bars to 21.2.
