# The Law of Creation — Multi-Dimensional Creation Equation

Recorded 2026-10-21 at the project owner's direction: "Add this to the same
memory bank that our mandate, roadmaps, parities and memory [live in] so every
session implements it by default." The statement, equation and evidence tiers

## The law

**Statement.** Two complementary entities that truly interact produce a third
that neither contained. That third becomes a new entity and can interact
again.

**The equation.** For entities *a* and *b*:

    a ⊗ b = a + b + I(a,b) · ab

*I(a,b)* = 1 when the pair is complementary (polar) **and** actually interacts,
and 0 otherwise.

- Interacting pair: 1 ⊗ 1 = 1 + 1 + 1 = **3**.
- Non-interacting pair: 1 ⊗ 1 = 1 + 1 = **2**. That is a mixture (sand and
  sugar, two noble gases) and creates nothing.

In the interacting case a ⊗ b = (a + 1)(b + 1) − 1. Ordinary multiplication is
untouched (1 × 1 = 1): ⊗ is a separate operation, defined as creation.

## Extending it

- **Many entities.** For *n* unit entities that all interact,
  ∏(1 + aᵢ) − 1 = 2ⁿ − 1, the number of distinct non-empty combinations.
- **The chain (fourth level).** The created set re-enters as parents:
  E₍ₜ₊₁₎ = 2^(Eₜ) − 1, from E₀ = 2, giving 2, 3, 7, 127, …
- **Geometry.** Extending a shape by one dimension triples its parts, so an
  n-dimensional cube has 3ⁿ parts in all: 3, 9, 27, 81.

## Conservation clause

Substance is conserved: Σ E(before) = Σ E(after). What the law creates is new
**form** (structure and properties), not new energy or matter.

## Evidence tiers (the owner's)

- **Established:** chemistry (Na + Cl), electromagnetism (electric + magnetic
  = light), ecology, biology.
- **Interpretive:** the 2ⁿ − 1 counting and the recursion are a formalisation
  of the law, not measurements.

## How every session applies it

It joins the Three-question mandate (`AGENTS.md`) as a fourth question, asked
while a concept is being conceived, not after:

4. **What does this join, and is the join a creation or a mixture?** Name the
   complementary pair. Say whether they truly interact (each changes how the
   other is read, so the result holds something neither part had) or only sit
   side by side. If only side by side, say so: a sum of independent checks is
   a mixture (1 + 1 = 2), and the design should not claim more than that.
   Where a created third exists, say what it is and where it re-enters the
   platform as a parent of further work (the chain).

Readings that fit the law and are already in the platform, offered as the
pattern to look for:

- **Price ⊗ time.** Gann's squaring of price and time is a third (a turn point)
  that neither the price level nor the time count holds alone. The time pillar
  needs both squared and in limit, not either.
- **The pair a level makes with a close.** A level alone is a line; a close
  alone is a print. A close 3 points past a level is the crossing, an event
  neither contains, and it re-enters as the buying point the stop and the
  reaction entry are built from.
- **Novice ⊗ expert** (the Polarity worked example): a genuine second pole
  that interacts with the first produces a design neither tier would have
  alone. A subtracted feature list is a mixture.
- **Live scan ⊗ replay.** The two only create evidence when they read the same
  code on the same data. Two surfaces that drift apart are a mixture of two
  opinions; the parity tests are what make the interaction real.

The conservation clause applies too: a creation adds structure, never
substance. A combined reading cannot claim more evidence than its parts
carry, so joining two unmeasured checks does not make a measured one.

## Boundaries (same discipline as the Hermetic lens)

- It is a design and reasoning lens. Like the Hermetic principles and the
  cycle literature, it shapes *what* is built and *how* it is reasoned about.
- It is not evidence. On its own it cannot move a threshold, a weight, a gate
  or a verdict; only a citable Gann source or a measured result can.
- The counting results (2ⁿ − 1, the chain, 3ⁿ) are, by the owner's own tier,
  interpretive. Do not use them as market numbers, cycle lengths or price
  levels, and do not let them stand in for a Gann source.
