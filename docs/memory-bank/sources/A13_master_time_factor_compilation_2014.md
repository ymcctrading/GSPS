## Bottom line

- **It does not disclose the Master Time Factor.** The compiler says so at the start: Gann kept its nature secret all his life, and what he said in plain words about it is very little. Every statement in the booklet says what the Master Time Factor *does*. None says *how*.
- So the parity row "the undisclosed Master Time Factor forecast: cannot be copied" (`GANN_PARITY_ROADMAP.md`) stands, now on a fuller record.
- What it adds:
  - when Gann first named it
  - what he claimed it does
  - how his words about it should be read
  - three corrections to our own notes (§6)
  - a list of dated calls he credited to it (§5)

## 1. When it was discovered

- **Earliest known use of the name:** a surviving letter of **July 9, 1927** to J. H. Spohn.
  - Gann offers a summer class opening August 2, where his "latest discovery", the Master Time Factor, will be taught.
  - It comes with instruction in "the seasonal trend of man", the subject of *Wall Street Stock Selector* (1930) pp. 35–38. That is a personal trend from a birth date, not a market rule.
- **Earlier, without the name:**
  - an advertisement of January 16, 1921 says stocks follow a regular cycle and mathematical curve, and a time factor he discovered tells him when they will be high or low
  - *Truth of the Stock Tape* (1923) p. 116 calls the time factor behind his annual forecasts the most important thing of all, and declines to give the secret away
- Discovered before *The Tunnel Thru the Air* was copyrighted (November 3, 1927).
- **August 8, 1908.** The link to that date is **Les Clemens'** (biographical sketch, Halliker's course, 1995).
  - Gann's own brochure ("Mathematical Formula for Market Predictions", p. 1) dates "one of his greatest mathematical discoveries" to that day but does not name it.
  - Correction C-A13-1 below.
- **"Originated in 1909."** This phrase comes from 1948–49 advertisements by W. D. Gann Research, Inc., the business Gann sold in 1947. It is his successors' wording and may be a guess.
- **A 1922 interview** (*Morning Telegraph*, December 17, 1922):
  - Gann says it took twenty years to learn the cause that produces effects according to time, and that the public is not ready for it.
  - His analogy is water forced above its level, which needs no force to fall back. Prices are pushed above or below their natural value, and the time factor tells in advance when those extremes must come, by supply and demand.

## 2. What Gann said it does (paraphrased)

- **Repeating cycles.** It tells months and years ahead when certain time cycles repeat and cause extreme highs and lows (*New Stock Trend Detector*, 1936, p. 136; already in A05).
- **Accumulation and distribution.** It tells when accumulation or distribution is under way and when stocks are near tops or bottoms (a letter reprinted in *Wall Street Stock Selector*, p. 7).
- **End of a cycle.** It tells when time cycles "run out" and stocks top (1933), and "when time is up" for a big advance or decline (1938).
- **Paired with "Cycle Theory"** throughout the ads ("Master Time Factor and Cycle Theory"). Also named alongside:
  - the Great Cycle
  - a "mathematical law of cycles"
  - "mathematical science"
  - rules "found in the Bible": *New Stock Trend Detector* p. 128 gives Chrysler's top of October 6, 1928 and its low of 5 on June 4, 1932 as a Bible time rule, unexplained
- **With supply, demand and volume.** A 1921 ad says his study of time and volume lets him determine movements in advance.
- **Price, Time, Volume and Velocity** (advertisement, July 3, 1938). When time is up, volume starts, velocity increases and prices move.
  - This repeats *How to Make Profits in Commodities* p. 56 (A08: time overbalances space and volume).
  - It also matches the 1954 five factors (A11) and the Master Three-Dimension Chart. It corroborates rules already on file and adds none.
- **The extent of a move** (a 1937 article quoted in the brochure "Learn Before You Lose", p. 11):
  - how far an advance goes came from "a theory based on the law of averages"
  - under certain circumstances, stocks rise or fall about the same number of points
  - This is a price claim with no rule given.
- **How it was sold:**
  - the "No. 3 Master Forecasting Method": Form Rules, Resistance Levels and Volume of Sales, plus the Master Time Factor and "a new way of forecasting by mathematical rules"
  - a $1,500 cotton or grain forecasting course said to contain it
  - *Face Facts America!* (1940) p. 47: the Master Forecasting Method includes the Master Time Factor and Cycle Theory
  - a 1938 ad offers a new "automatic trend indicator chart" and an "F.F.F. method" never taught before (neither defined)
  - Hearsay (J. Rensink, *Trader's World*, 2005): Gann offered to teach it privately for $5,000.
- **The 1953 Master Calculator lesson, p. 3**, tells the student to apply its rules with the Master Time Factor and geometric angles. The term appears nowhere else in that course.
  - The compiler reads this as a sign that buyers were told what it was privately.
  - Correction C-A13-3 below.

## 3. How to read Gann's words (the compiler's caution, adopted here)

- **"Time factor" is not always the Master Time Factor.** Gann writes "a time factor", "the time factor" and "time factors". Treat a mention as the Master Time Factor only where he names it.
- **"Factor" can mean a part of the market.** Time, price and volume are "the three most important factors" (*45 Years in Wall Street* p. 38; *Commodities* p. 56).
- **"Our Time Factor" in the January 1931 Hexagon lesson** (A2.1 Ch. 15B; reprinted in B15). There Gann uses the words for the count of 1° a month on the 45° angle (360 months = 30 years). It is a "time factor" in his own lesson, not named as the Master one.
- **"The time cycle is up" means the cycle has run out.** Gann stresses the end of a cycle. *Wall Street Stock Selector* (1930, appendix p. 17): once the time cycle is up, nothing stops the move, and action equals reaction in the opposite direction.

## 4. Statements on mathematics and astrology

- **January 5, 1919 interview** (*New York Herald Magazine*):
  - Gann says he predicts with geometry and mathematics, as an astronomer does.
  - He says there is much in the vibrations of the planets, but astrology fails unless it rests on mathematics and on a "great law" the ancients kept out of their books.
  - The compiler notes this does not repudiate astrology. He also reports, as hearsay, that Gann later hired L. Edward Johndro for astrological work.
  - Consistent with A2.3 and A12, and with AGENTS.md's astrology section. Nothing new to build.
- **"The Human Body" (January 17, 1931):** study the position of 7 × 7 = 49 on every permanent chart. Gann adds that astrological proof is not enough without numbers and geometry.
  - 49 is already a count in `lib/gann/timeCycles.ts` ([42, 49] days).
- ***Tunnel*** pp. 75, 77: cycle theory, "or harmonic analysis", plus a correct base or starting point. Already in A03.
- ***Commodities*** p. 32: every top or bottom stands in exact proportion to some other top or bottom (percentages of the highest price, the lowest price and the range). Already built.

## 6. Corrections to our own notes

- **C-A13-1 — the 1908 date is Clemens', not Gann's.** A11's note put "(the undisclosed 'Master Time Factor')" after Gann's "August 8, 1908 … greatest mathematical discoveries". Gann's brochure does not name the discovery. The earliest dated use of the name is July 9, 1927. Corrected in A11.
- **C-A13-2 — the November 1935 lesson does not name the Master Time Factor.**
  - Gann headed it "Forecasting", on lesson pages F-1 to F-21. The longer title, "Master Time Factor and Forecasting by Mathematical Rules", is applied to it by others.
  - Neither "Master Time Factor" nor "time factor" appears in it.
  - It does call the 60-year cycle the "Great Cycle – Master Time Period". Its cycle hierarchy is disclosed and built (`timeCycles.ts`, `macroCycle.ts`).
  - Calling it "the Master Time Factor's outer shell" is our inference, not Gann's statement. Corrected in A2.1's note and in the catalog.
- **C-A13-3 — the 1953 calculator lesson names the Master Time Factor once** (p. 3), without saying what it is. Added to A12.

## Three-question notes

1. **Gann:** Tier A for his own statements as cited, Tier B for the compiler's inferences. Not one rule, number or construction of the Master Time Factor is disclosed.
2. **Dewey:** The booklet is a list of claimed hits with no base rate, no misses and no fixed rule, so it clears none of Dewey's items. It is the clearest example in the files of why a dated claim is not evidence (C06: persistence after discovery, a significance test).
3. **Hermetic:** Cause and Effect, in Gann's own framing: the 1922 interview, the 1919 interview ("every market movement is the result of a cause"), and action equalling reaction. This framing shapes how we read him. It is not a source for anything we build.
