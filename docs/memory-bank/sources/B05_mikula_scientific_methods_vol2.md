## What the book argues (synthesis)

**The thesis.** Gann's advanced methods were astrological, and he hid them behind
"key words" in his published texts. Vol. 1 proposed *natural* as the key; Vol. 2
adds *wise* and restates *science/scientific* as a veil for *astrology*. Mikula
says the "truth without proof is only opinion" and offers six original Gann
charts as the proof.

**Ch. 1 — "The Law".** Mikula sorts Gann's uses of the word *law* into three
kinds: ordinary (legislation, supply and demand), quasi-scientific, and
spiritual. He reads the quasi-scientific one as "every celestial action has an
equal and opposite terrestrial reaction." He supports it with Gann's own lines
pairing *natural law* with *action and reaction*: tides, the seasons, panics
"balancing time with price", and "everything moves in cycles as a result of the
natural law of action and reaction". He also cites *Tunnel Thru the Air*'s "it
is not my aim to explain the cause of cycles."
*Assessment:* the Gann quotations are real (cross-checked against A2.1, A03 and
A09 notes). The reading of them as astrology is Mikula's inference.

**Ch. 2 — Price/longitude conversion, part 2.**
- **Conversion.** A planetary longitude maps to prices by adding multiples of
  360. For example, 38° corresponds to 38, 398, 758 and so on.
- **Gann's 1948 May soybean chart** carries the longitude paths of Jupiter and
  Mars as lines on a price chart. It also records opens as "o" and closes as
  "x", plus counts from tops and bottoms. Mikula calls this direct evidence
  that Gann plotted planetary longitudes on price charts.
- **"Natural" dates.** Two are claimed. U.S. Steel's 162½ low on
  1929-05-31 in *Stock Selector* matches heliocentric Mars at 12°30′ of a
  sign (162.5°). The 1925-04-16 rye low at 103 in the 1946 *Mechanical Method*
  matches a Mars–Pluto conjunction. Pluto was only discovered in 1930, so
  Mikula concludes Gann back-tested it.
- **The 1954 letter's planetary averages (a Gann quote).** Gann called the
  average of the six major planets, heliocentric and geocentric, the most
  powerful time/price resistance points. He called the five-planet average
  with Mars left out important too. There are four averages in all.
  - Averaging raw longitudes gaps when a planet wraps from 360° to 0°.
    Mikula smooths the average by adding 360 at the wrap and thinks Gann did
    the same. Gann does not say.
  - Mikula also shows a faster eight-planet geocentric average, which is his
    own extension.

**Ch. 3 — Square longitudes and the second key word, *wise*.**
- **The three "wise" soybean turns.** *Speculation: A Profitable Profession*
  (1954) marks three turns: 1953-08-20 low 239½, 1953-12-17 low, and
  1954-04-27 top 422. Mikula ties each to a Jupiter aspect:
  - trine Neptune, with Jupiter at 81° = 9²
  - sesquisquare Saturn
  - trine Neptune, with the midpoint at 144° = 12²
- **The claimed rule.** Aspects that form on square-number longitudes carry
  extra weight.
- **Corroboration.** The same Jupiter/Saturn aspects show up in the 1954
  letter and as a "13" written on the 1948 chart.

**Ch. 4 — Gann's use of the Circle Chart.** This is the most Gann-evidenced
chapter.
- **Life cycles (*Truth of the Stock Tape*, pp. 34–35).**
  - Gann says a man's life runs in cycles like stocks. There is a time to
    stop, rest and keep what you have made, and a time to press when luck
    turns.
  - Mikula reads the spring-1908 turn as Gann's Saturn return (born
    1878-06-06).
  - The behavioural half ("know when to quit; press when trades start
    winning") is Gann's own text. See the GSPS cross-reference below.
- **Eclipse timing.** In *Truth of the Stock Tape*'s 1907 panic, an eclipse
  came during the top and the crash came as Venus crossed the eclipse
  longitude. *Stock Selector* repeats this with a **first-trade horoscope**:
  U.S. Steel first traded 1901-03-28 at 42¾ (12°45′), there was an eclipse on
  that longitude, and the 1901-05-09 panic followed as Mercury crossed it.
- **Two original Gann charts** show he overlaid the planetary positions at
  several tops and bottoms on one wheel:
  - an egg horoscope with highs and lows from 1921–31
  - a rye Circle Chart with the highs of 1951-12-12 (220½) and 1947-02-21
    (335), and the 1946-05-07 turn
- **Circle Chart construction.** This part is Gann's, from the Master Egg
  Course and the 1954 letter.
  - **Numbering.** Rings of 24 numbers run counter-clockwise. The rye chart
    goes to 24 × 24 = 576, in 15° sections, so two sections make one sign.
  - **Other divisions.** The egg chart uses 72 divisions of 5°. Gann had no
    single fixed division.
  - **Square version.** A "square chart" is the same thing laid out in
    columns Gann called "cycles". The circle's spirals become 45° lines on
    it. It let him try any division, such as 12 per cycle or 17, quickly.
  - **Colour coding.** Lows are circled red and highs green, so "the price is
    at the same longitude of the previous high or low, or 90, 120, 180 from
    it" can be read directly. That is a Gann quote, and it is the one
    non-astrological, directly usable idea here: price levels at fixed
    angular separations from prior extremes on a 360-unit wheel.
  - **Fractional prices.** They are placed proportionally, not rounded. In
    the letter, 311¼ = 18°45′ Pisces. With ¼-cent ticks and 15° per cent, one
    tick is 3°45′, which Mikula treats as the orb.
  - **Offset.** Gann writes numbers *between* the degree lines: "300 = 30°
    Virgo, 302 = 30° Libra". Mikula shifts them to align.
  - **"Natural angle" (Master Egg Course).** The October Eggs all-time low,
    1500 on 1932-05-28, sat at the 60° price longitude, "7 days from the
    natural angle". The Sun was at 67°. Here "natural angles" means the
    Earth–Sun positions, 365¼ days to 360°.
  - **Master 360° Square of 12.** Gann's worked example is the 1920-12-28
    Eggs high of 60¢. He calls the crossing of two 45° lines at that price a
    "strong natural resistance level". Mikula notes Gann's "270°" is really
    the 240° line and ties it to heliocentric Mercury.
- **"Active angles" (1954 letter).**
  - **Gann's definition.** Prices and time periods where a major planet's
    longitude sits, or where the squares, triangles and oppositions to it
    fall. His example: Saturn at 8–9° Scorpio, plus 90°, gives 8–9° Aquarius,
    which is the price 309 for May Beans. His example trade: short the
    1953-12-02 high at 311¼, and cover on 1953-12-17 at 296 "because the
    price was down to the 45° angle from 44 on the monthly high/low chart".
  - **Mikula's added claim.** Active angles are the *first-trade* horoscope
    longitudes (soybean futures first traded 1936-10-05) while a transiting
    planet crosses or aspects them.
  - **Worked cases.** He works Gann's 1954 short (sold July Beans 412 on
    04-30, covered 370 on 05-12) and six 1993–95 soybean turns: 757½, 604,
    732½, 554½, 661 and 592. Each is placed within about one tick of a
    Circle Chart longitude.

## Methodological notes (for anyone tempted to build this)

- **Selection bias is the whole problem.** There are ten planets, several
  averages, first-trade longitudes, aspects of 0/45/90/120/135/180°, helio or
  geo, and an orb of 3°45′. With that many candidates almost any price lies
  within one tick of *something*. The six 1993–95 cases were chosen after the
  fact. Nothing here counts the misses. This fails Dewey's criteria outright
  (C06): no significance test, no out-of-sample persistence, no count of
  non-hits.
- **The key-word decoding cannot be falsified as used.** "Natural" appears
  about 25 times per book. Mikula keeps only the uses that name a specific
  date, then finds an astrological event near each.
- **Gann's own words are the valuable part.** The Circle/Square Chart
  construction, the 24-ring and 576 extent, the colour-coded extremes, the
  "same longitude or 90/120/180 from prior highs and lows" rule, and the
  45°-line crossing as resistance are all traceable to Gann's own course
  material.

## Three-question notes

1. **Gann tier.** The Circle Chart mechanics and the letter quotes are Gann
   (A2.3 for the letter; Master Egg Course sits within the A2 course family).
   Everything decoded is Tier B, Mikula.
2. **Dewey/cycles.** The periodicity claims (Saturn return, eclipses, aspect
   timing) clear none of Dewey's items as presented. There is no repetition
   count, no constancy test and no out-of-sample check.
3. **Hermetic.** Mikula's "celestial action, terrestrial reaction" is a direct
   statement of **Correspondence** ("as above, so below") and **Cause and
   Effect**. That is exactly why AGENTS.md's citation discipline matters here:
   the framing fits the lens perfectly, and it is still not evidence.
