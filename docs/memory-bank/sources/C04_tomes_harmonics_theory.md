## Content (synthesis)
- **Physics premise (speculative; not needed by GSPS):**
  - Everything is a wave, and matter is a standing wave.
  - Non-linearity, i.e. waves interacting through the metric or matter, makes all waves lose energy into harmonics.
  - The result is a hierarchy of harmonically related scales from cosmology down to atoms.
  - The theory requires an oscillating universe and rejects the Big Bang, which Tomes states openly.
- **C(H), the core mathematics.** C(H) is the number of **ordered factorisations** of H: the number of ways harmonic H is reached by repeated harmonic generation.
  - Examples: C(12) = 8; C(24) = 20.
  - C(H) rises steeply, so Tomes detrends it (roughly ÷H, later with a fitted Cest(H)) to expose the pattern.
- **"Main line" harmonics** (the strongest at each stage): **1, 2, 4, 12, 24, 48, 144, 288, 1440, 2880, 8640, 17280, 34560…**
  - Successive ratios are 2, 2, 3, 2, 2, 3, 2, 5, 2, 3, 2, 2, 2, 3, 2…
  - About **one ratio of 3 for every two to three ratios of 2** (≈ 2.38 on average in his larger computation).
  - Primes above 3 are rare.
  - Families branch off the main line by ×2 steps, and a ratio of 3 is like a musical modulation.
  - This corrects C01's early "always ratio 2" result.
- **Musical structure:** within an octave the dominant harmonics form the diatonic scale and common chords, as in C01.

- **Dewey's five conclusions** (from several thousand collected cycle reports):
  1. Everything that can be made into a time series has cycles.
  2. Common periods recur across unrelated fields.
  3. They show **synchrony** (common peaks and troughs).
  4. They stand in **simple ratios of 2 and 3**.
  5. Some are extraterrestrial.

## The Gann ↔ Tomes/Dewey bridge (key finding for the master report)
- Gann's number system is built from 2s and 3s:
  - divisions ½, ⅓, ¼, ⅛ (and ⅜, ⅝, ⅞)
  - master numbers 9, 12, 36, 72, 144
  - Square of 144 → **20,736 days** (144²)
  - 360 = 2³·3²·5; 90 = 2·3²·5
- Tomes's main line contains **12, 24, 48, 144, 288**. Gann's "Square of 144" and his use of 144 as a master number sit exactly on the strongest harmonic main line of the Tomes lattice.
- Gann's ranked time divisions (Master Course: anniversary > ½ > ¼ & ¾ > ⅓ & ⅔ > ⅛s) follow the same order a 2×3 lattice predicts: ratio-2 steps strongest, ratio-3 next, higher primes rare.
- ⇒ **Interpretation (ours, not a citation):**
  - Gann's divisions are an empirical harmonic analysis, which is what Gann himself called it ("cycle theory, or harmonic analysis," *Tunnel*). He wrote in 1926 that Schuster and Fourier theories would help (B01).
  - The cycle-theory literature supplies the **mechanism** (non-linear response ⇒ integer harmonics) and the **validation standard** (Dewey's checklist). Gann supplies the **rule**.
- **What this does not license:**
  - It does not make divisions Gann never used (e.g. ⅕ or ⅐ of a range) or any numerology admissible.
  - Numbers still need a Gann source (gate 1) and measurement (gate 2).
