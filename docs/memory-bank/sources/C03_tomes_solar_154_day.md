## Content (synthesis)
- **Rieger cycle:** a **154–155-day** period (range 140–170) in gamma-ray and proton flares, solar wind, the interplanetary magnetic field, sunspot areas and neutrino flux. First reported by Rieger et al. (Nature 1984).
  - It is strongest near solar maximum and fades at minimum. Tomes suggests re-analysing with **log data**, since skewed data may hide it.
- **Related periods:** 25.8-day rotation, seen as ~28 days from Earth; sub-harmonics 51.6, 77.4, 103.2 days; 78/84-day radio and flare periods; 1.28 years (= 3 × 156 d) and 2.14 years (= 5 × 156 d).
  - Neutrino flux shows 28.4 days with **annual side-bands** at 10.88–14.88 cycles/yr. The side-bands are evidence the signal is truly solar.
- **Modulation:** Nesterov's radio spectrum shows triplets around 154 and 77 days. Tomes reads these as amplitude modulation by ~4.7/9.4-year periods, likely the 11-year cycle, but the data covers only two solar cycles.
- **Harmonic table (days):** exact ×2 horizontally and ×3 vertically. Rows: 25.8, 51.6, 103.2; 77.4, 154.7, 309.5, 619; 464.2, 1238.
  - **1238 days = 40.68 months, Dewey/Mogey's stock-market cycle.**
  - **619 days = 1.695 years, Tomes's wheat cycle.**
  - The whole solar family is a factor of **7** below Dewey's table of common cycles.

## GSPS relevance
- A third independent anchor for the **~40.7-month (3.39-year) stock cycle**: Mogey/Dewey (B13), Lane 1950 (C01), and here, harmonically linked to solar periods. Documentation value only for GSPS's 3–4-year campaign counts.
- **Method note:** the "log data" suggestion matters for `spectralCycle.ts`. Prices are log-normal-ish, so detrending log closes before a spectral fit is the better default. Check what `spectralCycle.ts` currently does before claiming a gap.
- The **amplitude-modulation caution** (a cycle present near solar max and absent near min) parallels Gann's warning that cycles run "ahead of or behind time" (A11). Dominance and constancy can be **regime-dependent**.

## Three-question notes
1. **Gann:** not a source. Solar periods would fall under the astrology/astronomy boundary if used as a timing input. **Not admissible as a GSPS signal.** Recorded only as cross-series corroboration of a stock-market period GSPS already derives from Gann's own campaign statistics.
2. **Dewey checklist:**
   - Addressed: cross-series clustering (many solar observables; the stock/wheat matches) and dominance (the reported spectral peaks).
   - Constancy of period is **explicitly weak** (140–170 d, appears and disappears).
   - No phase-resumption or repetition-count test.
3. **Hermetic:**
   - **Correspondence**: the same 2×3 lattice at a different scale (÷7) links the Sun's rhythm to markets.
   - **Vibration**: rotation harmonics.
   - Not Cause and Effect: no mechanism for stocks is claimed here.
