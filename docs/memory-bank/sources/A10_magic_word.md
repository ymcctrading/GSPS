## Gann's own statements (paraphrased, with PDF page)
### Law, proof and the "one law, three applications"
- The Bible is "a book of laws; physical, natural, mental, spiritual and **time** laws" (p.41). Obedience brings reward, disobedience punishment — **here and now**, not after death (p.8, 41).
- **"Prove all things and hold fast to that which is good"** (p.8) — the same prove-before-trust stance he takes with trading rules (cf. A05 Ch. II "prove the rules to yourself").
- Sowing and reaping is "a natural law of cause and effect — everything brings forth after its own kind" (p.42). You cannot get something good for nothing; "you must give to receive" (p.11–12).
- **Three kinds of laws, or three applications of the one law** — spiritual law to spiritual things, natural/physical law to the body, **the law of finance to financial things**; "the law is the same… you reap just what you sow"; obeying one while breaking another still produces loss in that domain (p.111). ⇒ Gann's own explicit statement of **Correspondence** (one law, many planes).
- "The thing that hath been, it is that which shall be… there is no new thing under the sun" (Eccl. 1:9, quoted p.106) and "To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose" (Eccl. 3:1–8, p.106); Gann: "There is a time and place for everything. This is a law laid down in the Bible" (p.107). ⇒ the scriptural basis Gann himself cites for *history repeats* and *time governs* (cf. A05 "History Repeats"; A3 *Tunnel*).

### Vibration, sound and number
- Words are spirit and vibration; the spoken word "vibrated into the air" returns (p.105). Radio analogy: sound waves once set in motion keep moving and are received by any set **tuned to the same wave length** (pp.74–75, 107). ⇒ Gann's own description of **resonance** (the tuned-receiver idea behind his "Law of Vibration").
- **Sound can build or destroy**: walls of Jericho after marching **seven times in seven days**; a violinist claiming the right key could bring down the Brooklyn Bridge; glass shattered by sound; the body disorganised by discordant sound and healed by harmonious music; "everyone's body contains a harmonious keynote" (p.112). ⇒ explicit **resonance / keynote** doctrine (Vibration).
- Harmony words: Jehovah preferred; also OH HO, AH HA, AUM/OM, LOVE, ELOHIM, JAH, DO RE MI — "get your chord or keynote" (p.62).
- **Number symbolism (Gann's own):** Jehovah = 3 syllables, 7 letters; "three is referred to more times in the Bible than any other number except the sacred number seven" (p.108). "I AM GOD, LOVE" = 10 letters, "ten is one of the Perfect numbers" (p.109). YOD HE VAU HE = 4 syllables, 10 letters; four syllables = working "into the fourth dimension" (p.110). GOD, I AM, AUM = 3 letters (p.110).
- **The circle:** "O, or the circle… has no beginning and no ending, it is everlasting… includes the world and everything in it because it vibrates in a circle" (pp.109–110).
- **Hexagonal packing construction (p.108):** seven pennies/circles — one in the centre, six around it — then a seventh, enclosing circle drawn round the rim of the six; place "O" at the centre of JEHOVAH with the other letters round it = "the sacred circle." ⇒ The 1-surrounded-by-6 hexagon is the same geometry as Gann's **Hexagon Chart** (A2.1 Ch. 15B) — here stated as a sacred construction, not a price tool.
- **Repetition schedule:** repeat the Word **three times a day for at least seven days**, better **three × seven = 21 days** (p.109); morning, noon and night (Ps. 55:17) (p.109, 113). Chant each syllable ~1 minute (p.113).

### Health, breath, fasting, time
- **"Your most valuable possession is TIME. You exchange time for money"**; next is good health; money comes through knowledge (p.87).
- **Twelve breaths for twelve body regions** (head, throat, lungs, stomach, heart, bowels, back/loins & solar plexus, genital organs, hips/thighs, knees, calves, feet); cites Hindu practice of 3 and 7 breaths / 3×3=9 over three periods (pp.88–89). Own practice: 7 deep breaths, rest, ×3 = 21 (p.114).
- Solar plexus = "your superior brain… gets you in tune with all the unseen forces" (p.109).
- Life needs breath (minutes) > water (~4 days) > food (30–40 days) (p.114).
- **Fast four times per year at the change of the seasons, 3–7 days each**; ideally one day a week (p.115). Personal account: 1943 sciatica cured by a **nine-day fast** (p.116); began the book in 1943; finished after retiring from active business. ⇒ Gann marks the **seasonal quarter points** as personal renewal dates (cf. seasonal time periods in A2.1 Ch. 5/17 and `lib/gann/timeCycles.ts`).
- Sun baths; sun, water, earth needed for growth — sun (heat) most important (pp.89–90).
- "Stagnation is death and action is life" (p.115).
- Aged 72 at writing (p.119).

### Behavioural/financial observations relevant to trading psychology
- **Fear is the father of jealousy, anger, hatred and wrong thinking**; remove fear first (p.59). Fear and doubt "short-circuit" mind and body (pp.66–67, 75, 113).
- **Anecdote: a Wall Street trader who made >$1 million by study and right rules, wanted 2–3 million, and lost it all trying to make money too fast** (p.63). ⇒ Gann's own warning against greed/over-trading after success.
- **Anecdote: two sisters refused $125,000, then $75,000, for a house because they had fixed on $125,000; later lost it for taxes** (p.64). ⇒ fixed price ideas ("having your own way") are ruinous — the same point as "don't fix a price target in your head" (A2.1 Ch. 1) and anchoring bias.
- Buy only what you *need* (worked family budget anecdote, p.63). Give ~10% (tithe) (p.68). Don't dwell on past losses ("let the dead past bury the dead"; Lot's wife) (pp.64–65). Anger destroys judgment (p.65). "Confusion": you cannot think of two things at once — concentrate (p.67). Write mistakes down and burn the paper (confession) (p.66).
- Intuition/"inner eye" vs. the eye's illusions (rails appear to meet) (p.76).

### World problems (Ch. VI, pp.70–72)
- Wars recur "every twenty or thirty years" and never solve problems (p.70); rulers start wars to cover financial mistakes. (Cf. A05's statement that the 20-year panic cycle and wars are linked: "the love of money will be the cause of the next war.")
