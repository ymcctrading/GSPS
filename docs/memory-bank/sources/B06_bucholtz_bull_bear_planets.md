## Content
- **Ch. 1:**
  - geocentric vs heliocentric zodiac basics
  - aspects 0/30/45/60/90/120/150/180 with stated **orbs** (10° for conjunction and opposition, 5° for squares, sextiles and trines, 3° for semi-sextiles and semi-squares)
  - the NYSE "birth" May 17 1792 (the same origin Gann used for his NYSE Square-of-20 chart, Master Course Ch. 15A)
- **Ch. 2, lunar:**
  - stocks whose **first trade date** fell on a new or full Moon are said to turn at new and full Moons, and at apogee and perigee
  - North Node sign changes (18.6-year nodal cycle, ≈ every 1.55 years)
  - eclipses (eclipse seasons 173.3 days apart)
  - all illustrated with a few hand-picked charts plus RSI/DMI/CCI confirmation
- **Ch. 3, planetary:**
  - build a **first-trade horoscope** for each stock, then mark its aspects on past charts, then keep the ones that "align" (five steps)
  - exaltation, fall and detriment degrees
  - Mercury, Venus and Mars crossing the chart's MC/Ascendant
  - retrogrades (Mercury ×3 per year; Venus; Jupiter, with a table showing *small* net S&P moves during Jupiter retrogrades)
  - sign changes (Neptune, Uranus)
  - **synodic periods**, S = s₁s₂/(s₂−s₁); tables include **Jupiter–Saturn 19.85 years**
  - Jupiter–Saturn 0/90/180° vs Dow turns (1961, 1970–71, 1975–76, 1981, 2000, 2006, 2010–11)
  - subdivisions of 19.85 years (÷4 = 59.6 months … ÷9 = 26.5 months) projected from the Oct 2002 low
  - Saturn–Uranus 45 yr (1929 → 1974?)
- **Ch. 4, commodities:** first-trade charts for gold, silver, copper, crude, grains (heliocentric Mercury–Saturn and Mercury–Jupiter from 1970s literature), soy complex, currencies, cattle, hogs, lumber, bonds. Aspect dates are overlaid on 6–12-month charts.
- Appendices: 2013–14 lunar, retrograde and aspect date tables, and first-trade dates.

## Assessment
- **Method = post-hoc visual alignment** on short samples, with generous orbs (±3–10°, "within a few days"), many planet pairs and many aspect angles, and selection of whichever aspects "align most often". A textbook **multiple-comparisons / look-elsewhere** design. **No statistics, no out-of-sample test, no base rate.** The Jupiter-retrograde table is the only one reporting non-events.
- **Nothing here is Gann-sourced.** Where it overlaps with Gann it is by analogy:
  - **"First trade date" ↔ Gann's incorporation date** as a per-stock time origin (Master Course Ch. 7; *Tunnel* Ch. XVI's "age" of Major Motors). Gann used the incorporation date for **time counts** (anniversaries, fractions of cycles), not horoscopes.
  - **Jupiter–Saturn synodic 19.85 years ↔ Gann's 20-year cycle.** A plausible physical correlate of a Gann period (Gann called 20 years the most important in 1931, per the catalog). This is the "cause" question Gann said he kept secret (B01).
  - **Saturn's 29.42-year orbit ↔ Gann's 30-year cycle** ("caused by the planet Saturn" in the course passage quoted in B01).
