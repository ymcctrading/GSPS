## Content (synthesis)
- **Part 1**
  - Restates Gann's 1909 *Ticker* claims: a periodic or cyclic law behind all price movement, and the **Law of Vibration** as the means to find the points to which prices rise and fall "within a given time."
  - Also: deal with *causes*, and "no chance in nature… exact proportion and perfect relationship."
  - Meadors traces Gann's squaring of price and time, and his divisions, to **Pythagoras' harmonic intervals** and the "Music of the Spheres."
  - He argues that "regular" time cycles are really irregular because calendar time is Earth/Sun based. He contrasts heliocentric (regular) with geocentric (irregular) planetary cycles.
  - Claims Gann's astro work was **empirical**, citing Gann's line that the vibratory forces "can only be known by the movements they generate."
  - Worked claim: the Oct 19 1987 low matched Uranus's 84-year return to its Nov 9 1903 position.
- **Part 2**
  - Newsletter forecasts presented as hits: an eclipse low in Nov 1993, a Dow 3000 top on Jul 16 1990, the Aug 1987 "Harmonic Convergence" top.
  - Cites **Dewey, "Stock Prices and Space," *Cycles*, Oct 1969**: DJIA 1897–1961, monthly changes around geocentric conjunctions and oppositions of certain planets by sky segment. Reported: advances 92% of the time near the "Galactic Center," and advances in the 30 days before Mars/Jupiter and Mercury conjunctions "surely not chance."
  - Meadors calls his forecast "100% accurate."
- **Part 3**
  - More self-reported hits (Mar–May 1994, a 20:1 options return, the Jan 1991 solar-eclipse low).
  - Adds the fuller 1909 Gann quote: an original impulse resolves into periodic, rhythmical motion (pendulum, moon, seasons, the periodic table), and "the driving forces controlling the stocks are also in a state of vibration."
  - Claims all of Gann's other methods are "subservient" to the Law of Vibration.

## GSPS relevance
- **Nothing to build.** Every technique here is astrological timing (eclipses, planetary returns, conjunctions by sky segment). Under AGENTS.md "Astrology," that is never gating, and admissible as confluence only if traceable to Gann's own sources. Meadors's methods are his own ("our Harmonic Indicator Method"), not Gann's.
- **The accuracy claims** (100%, 20:1, "uncanny") are self-reported, cherry-picked newsletter hits: no denominator of misses, no out-of-sample test. They fail Dewey's criteria 3, 7 and 10 (C06). The banned-terms rule forbids repeating them as claims.
- **The Dewey 1969 "Stock Prices and Space" citation is worth recording.** Dewey did publish planetary–stock correlation work. It is not in our catalogue and has not been read. By Dewey's own C06 standard, a single in-sample study is a hypothesis. It does not change the Astrology policy.
- **Correct and useful:** the 1909 Gann quote on impulse becoming periodic motion is the clearest statement of Gann's **Rhythm/Vibration** premise and matches the A01 notes. Cite A01 (the primary), not Meadors.
- **Contested framing:** "all Gann's methods are subservient to the Law of Vibration" is Meadors's reading. Gann's published rules (A05, A2.1, A8) stand on their own mechanics (swings, levels, time counts) without it.

## Three-question notes
1. Tier B, promotional. Cite only for the Gann quotes, and cite them to A01.
2. Dewey: fails repetition count, out-of-sample and significance. The Dewey 1969 study it cites is an unread single study, a hypothesis at most.
