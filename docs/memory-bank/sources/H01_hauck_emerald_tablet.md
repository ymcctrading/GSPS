## Important attribution correction
The "seven Hermetic principles" AGENTS.md uses (Mentalism, Correspondence, Vibration, Polarity, Rhythm, Cause and Effect, Gender) are the framework of ***The Kybalion*** **(1908, "Three Initiates")**, not of Hauck. Hauck's own framework, in the chapters read, is:
- the Emerald Tablet's **rubrics**
- **One Mind / One Thing**
- **Above / Below**
- **four elements**
- **seven alchemical operations**

## Content (synthesis of chapters read)
- **Provenance legends (Ch. 2):**
  - Three "Hermes": Thoth; Akhenaten (in esoteric accounts); Balinas (Apollonius of Tyana).
  - Alexander finds the tablet at Siwa (332–331 BCE).
  - Earliest surviving text is an **Arabic appendix, c. 650–800 CE** (*Book of Balinas the Wise on Causes*; Jabir). It reached Latin Europe via the *Secretum Secretorum*, translated 1140/1243.
  - Hauck presents the older legends as legend. For GSPS the relevant historical fact is: **Arabic text c. 650–800 CE, Latin 12th century.**
- **Reading the tablet (Ch. 4).** Hauck divides it into **seven rubrics**. The Latin text used by Jung is given in a footnote (public domain).
  1. **Introductory**: truth "without deceit." Hauck reads it as the verifiable, experimental attitude: free of dogma and ego.
  2. **Orientation**, the **Doctrine of Correspondences**: Below corresponds to Above and Above to Below, "to accomplish the miracles of the One Thing." All things come from the One Thing through the meditation of the One Mind, "through Transformation" (adaptation).
     - Hauck insists the correspondence runs **both ways** ("As Below, so Above; as Above, so Below"). The popular one-way reading "as above, so below" is **half the equation**, and **work begins in the Below**.
     - The picture is a flow (Ouroboros, yin-yang), not a hierarchy that discards the lower level.
     - There are no real miracles, only hidden laws not yet understood.
  3. **The One Thing** is the subject of the whole tablet. It is formless potential, the alchemists' **First Matter** (84 synonyms in Ruland's 1612 *Lexicon*): overlooked, "a stone and no stone," found everywhere and prized by no one. Hauck identifies it psychologically with **True Imagination**, as opposed to mere fantasy.
- **The third rubric (Chs. 5–6): four elements generated from the One Thing,** each paired with an operation:

| Tablet phrase | Element | Operation | Psychological reading (Hauck) |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Its father is the Sun" | Fire | **Calcination** | Burning off ego and dross, often by life's reverses |
| "Its mother the Moon" | Water | **Dissolution** | Letting rigid structures dissolve; receptivity |
| "The Wind carries it in its belly" | Air | **Separation** | Stepping back to discern essence from non-essence. **"It must occur at the proper moment or all is lost."** |
| "Its nurse is the Earth" | Earth | **Conjunction** | Union of opposites into a new third thing; "perfected if turned into Earth," i.e. principle must become embodied |

  - The remaining operations (from the table of contents, chapters not available): **Fermentation, Distillation, Coagulation**. That makes seven operations, with Calcination and Dissolution also treated in Chs. 11–12.
  - The elements are defined by **qualities** (hot/cold × dry/moist, after Aristotle). One element changes into another by **changing one quality** (water → air by exchanging cold for hot). Jung maps them to thinking (Fire), intuition (Air), feeling (Water) and sensation (Earth).
  - **Sun/Moon polarity:** the Sun is steady and the Moon keeps its own **28-day cycle** and "irregular" motion; White Sun / Black Moon are both necessary.
  - Heraclitus via Hauck: everything becomes fire and is born from fire, i.e. **transformation, not creation or destruction**.
- **Worked biographies** illustrate each stage: Calcination (William Shatner), Dissolution (Teresa of Ávila), Separation (Cat Stevens / Yusuf Islam). Anecdotal; nothing for GSPS.
- Hauck's register is devotional/paranormal (UFOs, levitation). **None of it is evidence** under GSPS's citation discipline. It is a **design lens only**.

## What GSPS can take from it (lens, not evidence)
1. **Two-way Correspondence.** The platform's cross-timeframe and cross-surface consistency rule (AGENTS.md "Cross-platform consistency") is Correspondence in one direction: a concept proven in one place must exist everywhere it applies. Hauck's reading adds the other direction:
   - **evidence from the lower level (a single trade, the intraday bar, a novice's experience) must be allowed to revise the higher level (the method, the thresholds)**
   - and **work begins in the Below**
   - That is exactly what the measurement gate does: trade outcomes correct our translation of Gann's rules (AGENTS.md "Gann-derived AND measured"). This is a framing that justifies existing practice, not a new rule.
2. **Separation "at the proper moment."** A ready-made framing for Gann's **time** discipline: when to step aside or exit matters as much as what. Gann's time-over-price rule, counter-trend duration windows and "wait for the time to run out" map to Separation's timing warning. **Framing only.**
3. **The operations as a sequence of transformations**, not a linear pipeline that stops: Calcination → Dissolution → Separation → Conjunction → Fermentation → Distillation → Coagulation. Distillation is iterated, and the whole is a cycle (Ouroboros). This matches AGENTS.md's "Cycles as architecture" (a returning wheel, not drain-and-halt) and is a natural frame for the **trade lifecycle** (scan → separate candidates → conjoin criteria → confirm → hold → exit → review → rescan) and for **tier ascension** (Polarity audit; three-path promotion).
4. **One element becomes another by changing one quality.** A useful design metaphor for *state machines* (regime or lifecycle states): transitions defined by a single changed condition are more legible than wholesale jumps. `lib/signals/states/*` and `lib/lifecycle/*` already work this way. Again, framing, not evidence.
5. **The overlooked First Matter.** "Found everywhere, prized by no one" is the right attitude to the plainest Gann tools (the 50% point, the swing top and bottom, the calendar anniversary), which carry more of the method than elaborate constructions do. This matches GANN_METHODOLOGY_FULL_REPORT §18.4's "cheapest / most literal Gann rules first."
