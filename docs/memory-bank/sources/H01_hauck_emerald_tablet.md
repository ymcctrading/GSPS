## Important attribution correction
The "seven Hermetic principles" AGENTS.md uses (Mentalism, Correspondence, Vibration, Polarity, Rhythm, Cause and Effect, Gender) are the framework of ***The Kybalion*** **(1908, "Three Initiates")**, not of Hauck. Hauck's own framework, in the chapters read, is:
- the Emerald Tablet's **rubrics**
- **One Mind / One Thing**
- **Above / Below**
- **four elements**
- **seven alchemical operations**

`GANN_HISTORICAL_SOURCES.md` already words this correctly ("popularized in *The Kybalion* and carried forward in Hauck's alchemical writing"). The Kybalion is not in the reviewed catalog. If the seven principles are to be cited *by text*, the Kybalion should be added (it is public domain in the US, published 1908).

## Content (synthesis of chapters read)
- **Provenance legends (Ch. 2):**
  - Three "Hermes": Thoth; Akhenaten (in esoteric accounts); Balinas (Apollonius of Tyana).
  - Alexander finds the tablet at Siwa (332–331 BCE).
  - Earliest surviving text is an **Arabic appendix, c. 650–800 CE** (*Book of Balinas the Wise on Causes*; Jabir). It reached Latin Europe via the *Secretum Secretorum*, translated 1140/1243.
  - Hauck presents the older legends as legend. For GSPS the relevant historical fact is: **Arabic text c. 650–800 CE, Latin 12th century.**
- **Reading the tablet (Ch. 4).** Hauck divides it into **seven rubrics**. The Latin text used by Jung is given in a footnote (public domain).
  1. **Introductory**: truth "without deceit." Hauck reads it as the verifiable, experimental attitude: free of dogma and ego.
  2. **Orientation**, the **Doctrine of Correspondences**: Below corresponds to Above and Above to Below, "to accomplish the miracles of the One Thing." All things come from the One Thing through the meditation of the One Mind, "through Transformation" (adaptation).
     - Hauck insists the correspondence runs **both ways** ("As Below, so Above; as Above, so Below"). The popular one-way reading "as above, so below" is **half the equation**, and **work begins in the Below**.
     - The picture is a flow (Ouroboros, yin-yang), not a hierarchy that discards the lower level.
     - There are no real miracles, only hidden laws not yet understood.
  3. **The One Thing** is the subject of the whole tablet. It is formless potential, the alchemists' **First Matter** (84 synonyms in Ruland's 1612 *Lexicon*): overlooked, "a stone and no stone," found everywhere and prized by no one. Hauck identifies it psychologically with **True Imagination**, as opposed to mere fantasy.
- **The third rubric (Chs. 5–6): four elements generated from the One Thing,** each paired with an operation:

| Tablet phrase | Element | Operation | Psychological reading (Hauck) |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Its father is the Sun" | Fire | **Calcination** | Burning off ego and dross, often by life's reverses |
| "Its mother the Moon" | Water | **Dissolution** | Letting rigid structures dissolve; receptivity |
| "The Wind carries it in its belly" | Air | **Separation** | Stepping back to discern essence from non-essence. **"It must occur at the proper moment or all is lost."** |
| "Its nurse is the Earth" | Earth | **Conjunction** | Union of opposites into a new third thing; "perfected if turned into Earth," i.e. principle must become embodied |

  - The remaining operations, Fermentation, Distillation and Coagulation, are covered in Chs. 15–17 (see below). That makes seven.
  - The elements are defined by **qualities** (hot/cold × dry/moist, after Aristotle). One element changes into another by **changing one quality** (water → air by exchanging cold for hot). Jung maps them to thinking (Fire), intuition (Air), feeling (Water) and sensation (Earth).
  - **Sun/Moon polarity:** the Sun is steady and the Moon keeps its own **28-day cycle** and "irregular" motion; White Sun / Black Moon are both necessary.
  - Heraclitus via Hauck: everything becomes fire and is born from fire, i.e. **transformation, not creation or destruction**.
- **Worked biographies** illustrate each stage: Calcination (William Shatner), Dissolution (Teresa of Ávila), Separation (Cat Stevens / Yusuf Islam). Anecdotal; nothing for GSPS.
- Hauck's register is devotional/paranormal (UFOs, levitation). **None of it is evidence** under GSPS's citation discipline. It is a **design lens only**.

## Chs. 7–25 (synthesis, read 2026-09-27)
**Part 1, end (Chs. 7–9): the rest of the tablet.**
- **Ch. 7, "Separate the Earth from Fire":** Separation continued. The subtle is separated from the gross "gently and with great judgment." Hauck reads this as discrimination, not violence.
- **Ch. 8, the "Pyramid Rubric":**
  - The fourth rubric, seven lines, is named for its structure: the four elements form the base, and three heavenly substances act on them — **Sulfur** (soul/will), **Mercury** (spirit/mind) and **Salt** (body/fixed form).
  - These converge on a single apex (the "One-Point").
  - Its first half describes an ascent and its second half a descent back into matter: **Distillation**, rising and returning repeatedly.
  - **Pernety's four grades of Fire** (1758) run from the elementary fire of stoves up to the white Celestial Fire. The real fire is internal to the matter, not applied from outside.
- **Ch. 9, "This Is the Pattern":** the closing rubrics.
  - The whole operation is a template that applies at every scale.
  - **Solve et Coagula** (dissolve and coagulate) is the "Emerald Formula" summarizing all seven operations.

**Part 2 (Chs. 10–21): the seven operations as a working method.**
- **Ch. 10:** the seven steps as a ladder.
  - Each step is associated with a planet, a metal and (in places) a zodiac sign.
  - An **"eighth step"** lies beyond the seventh: a return and completion, not a further rung ("the Eighth is a complete circle," quoted from a Hermetic tractate).
  - The initials of the Latin motto *Visita Interiora Terrae Rectificando Invenies Occultum Lapidem* spell **VITRIOL**, a mnemonic for the whole process.
- **Chs. 11–17:** one chapter per operation (Calcination, Dissolution, Separation, Conjunction, Fermentation, Distillation, Coagulation). Each has:
  - a chemical description
  - a psychological reading
  - a meditation or exercise
  - a biography illustrating it
- **Recurring points in Chs. 11–17:**
  - **Fermentation** is the introduction of new life into the conjoined matter after a "putrefaction" (black phase). An apparent death precedes the living growth.
  - **Distillation** is repeated purification by rising and condensing, many times over. The operation is iterative, not one pass.
  - **Coagulation** is the embodiment of the result as a stable new form: the Stone, *ultima materia*.
  - **Maria Prophetissa** (Alexandrian alchemist) is credited with the water bath and the still. **"Maria's axiom" ("One becomes two, two becomes three, and out of the third comes the one as the fourth")** is used as the structural rule for how opposites generate a third that completes a whole.
  - **Eudoxus of Cnidus** is cited on sublimation. A footnote says his *Oktaeteris* (c. 370 BCE) proposes an **eight-year cycle**.
    - Historical note, not from Hauck: the octaeteris is the Greek **8-year luni-solar calendar cycle** (8 solar years ≈ 99 lunations).
    - It is a calendar-reconciliation period, not a market or "cosmic" cycle. Hauck's gloss that Eudoxus was "being initiated into the Hermetic mysteries" is unsupported.
- **Ch. 18:** health and longevity. Anecdote and devotional material; nothing for GSPS.
- **Ch. 19, "Message of the Caduceus":**
  - The seven operations are mapped onto a vertical **caduceus ladder** from base to crown: **Saturn–lead, Jupiter–tin, Mars–iron, Venus–copper, Mercury–quicksilver, Moon–silver, Sun–gold**.
  - Hauck equates these with the seven chakras.
  - A late-antique source (via a Roman author on Persian mysteries) gives "a ladder with seven gates and an eighth above."
  - *Note:* this is the traditional **planet–metal correspondence**, the same one Gann-adjacent astrological writers use when they tie commodity metals to planets. See the Kolisko item below.
- **Ch. 20:** the "chemistry of miracles" and the **Arcanum Experiment** (after Gottlieb Latz's *Die Alchemie*).
- **Ch. 21:** the formula in everyday life, and the **Personal Alchemy Quiz**, a self-assessment of which operation one is "stuck" in.

**Part 3 (Chs. 22–25): the future.**
- **Ch. 22, "The Hermes Factor":** the tablet's influence on Western history — Renaissance Hermeticism, Freemasonry, the Rosicrucians and the American founding.
- **Chs. 23–25:** millennial speculation, "have you seen the Stone," and closing aphorisms.

### Gann-relevant item: planet–metal and metal prices (Ch. 19, book p. 308)
- **What Hauck says:**
  - Hauck reports that **Lilly Kolisko** (*Workings of the Stars in Earthly Substance*, 1926) used capillary/filter-paper chromatography of metal-salt solutions and reported pattern changes at planetary conjunctions and oppositions involving the metal's "ruling" planet (Moon–Mars with iron/silver; Saturn with lead; Jupiter with tin).
  - He adds, citing **Nick Kollerstrom, *The Metal-Planet Relationship* (1993)**, that copper and gold price fluctuations "have been statistically correlated" to lunar and solar conjunctions with the planets, with studies reportedly published in *Metal Bulletin*, *Financial Weekly* and the *Krugerrand Bulletin*.
- **Status for GSPS: a pointer, not evidence.**
  - Hauck gives no data, method, sample, or base rate.
  - Kolisko's experiments are not accepted replications in mainstream chemistry.
  - The price studies are cited third-hand.
  - It does **not** meet Dewey's checklist or the AGENTS.md citation discipline.
- **Why it's recorded:**
  - It is the only market-price claim in the book.
  - It uses the same planet–metal table (Saturn–lead … Sun–gold) that bears on the astrology research track (master report AS items).
  - If that track ever tests planet-to-commodity links, Kollerstrom 1993 is the source to obtain and evaluate directly, not Hauck.

### Unreliable history (flag, do not cite)
- The claim that **50 of the 56 signers of the Declaration of Independence were Masons** (Ch. 22) is a well-known exaggeration. Mainstream estimates are roughly 9 to about a dozen confirmed.
- The linked claims about Washington "openly supporting" the Illuminati are unsupported.
- Hauck's datings of early alchemical history (the Diocletian decree against alchemy, legendary provenance of the tablet) should be treated as legend unless checked against scholarly sources.
- None of this affects GSPS directly. It is recorded so no future session repeats it as fact.

## Additional lens points from Chs. 7–25
6. **Solve et Coagula as the lifecycle.** Dissolve (break a market's history into swings, levels and time counts) and coagulate (recombine into one plan). It is iterated, not one pass — Distillation's repeated rising and condensing. This supports treating each trade review as input to the next scan (see "Cycles as architecture").
7. **Maria's axiom, 1 → 2 → 3 → 4.** A pattern for Polarity resolved into a third. Two opposed poles (novice/expert, price/time, buy/sell) produce a synthesis that completes the whole. It is a framing for Gann's **price-and-time squaring**, where price and time are two poles and the "square" is the third that marks the change. Framing only.
8. **The eighth step returns to the first.** The Octad is completion as return, the same shape as Gann's "time repeats": the cycle's completion is the start of the next. Consistent with the rotation design in `lib/scan/universe-rotation.ts`.
9. **The fire is internal (Pernety).** The driving force is in the matter, not applied from outside. It is a lens for preferring signals drawn from the market's own swings, ranges and time counts over imposed external indicators, which restates the Gann-grounded platform principle.

## What GSPS can take from it (lens, not evidence)
1. **Two-way Correspondence.** The platform's cross-timeframe and cross-surface consistency rule (AGENTS.md "Cross-platform consistency") is Correspondence in one direction: a concept proven in one place must exist everywhere it applies. Hauck's reading adds the other direction:
   - **evidence from the lower level (a single trade, the intraday bar, a novice's experience) must be allowed to revise the higher level (the method, the thresholds)**
   - and **work begins in the Below**
   - That is exactly what the measurement gate does: trade outcomes correct our translation of Gann's rules (AGENTS.md "Gann-derived AND measured"). This is a framing that justifies existing practice, not a new rule.
2. **Separation "at the proper moment."** A ready-made framing for Gann's **time** discipline: when to step aside or exit matters as much as what. Gann's time-over-price rule, counter-trend duration windows and "wait for the time to run out" map to Separation's timing warning. **Framing only.**
3. **The operations as a sequence of transformations**, not a linear pipeline that stops: Calcination → Dissolution → Separation → Conjunction → Fermentation → Distillation → Coagulation. Distillation is iterated, and the whole is a cycle (Ouroboros). This matches AGENTS.md's "Cycles as architecture" (a returning wheel, not drain-and-halt) and is a natural frame for the **trade lifecycle** (scan → separate candidates → conjoin criteria → confirm → hold → exit → review → rescan) and for **tier ascension** (Polarity audit; three-path promotion).
4. **One element becomes another by changing one quality.** A useful design metaphor for *state machines* (regime or lifecycle states): transitions defined by a single changed condition are more legible than wholesale jumps. `lib/signals/states/*` and `lib/lifecycle/*` already work this way. Again, framing, not evidence.
5. **The overlooked First Matter.** "Found everywhere, prized by no one" is the right attitude to the plainest Gann tools (the 50% point, the swing top and bottom, the calendar anniversary), which carry more of the method than elaborate constructions do. This matches GANN_METHODOLOGY_FULL_REPORT §18.4's "cheapest / most literal Gann rules first."
