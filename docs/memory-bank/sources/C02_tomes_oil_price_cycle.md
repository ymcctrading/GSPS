## Content (synthesis)
- **Data:** WTI spot oil (FRED), 1946–2005.
- **Period-estimation method, worth copying as a technique.**
  - Measure the period from **symmetric pairs of peaks**: first with last (10 cycles), second with second-to-last (8), and so on. Skip a missing peak.
  - Each pair gives 5.44–5.61 years. The lone single-cycle pair is the outlier at 6.25.
  - The **weighted grand average** (total years ÷ total cycles = 155.08 / 28) is **5.539 years**, stated as **5.54 ± 0.03**.
- **Phase-estimation method.**
  - Project the next peak from **every** past peak by adding n × 5.54 years, then **average the projections**.
  - Result: next peak 2008-01 (it actually occurred mid-2008), then 2013-07 and 2019-02.
  - Mean timing error 6 months overall, 4 months after the price-controlled era.
- **Earlier Dewey oil reports** (3.5, 6 and 8 years) were based on only 2–3 cycles and are judged unreliable.
- The same ~5.5-year period is reported in wheat, stocks, hogs, corn, cocoa, cotton and post-office revenue. It is **exactly ½ of the 11.08-year sunspot average**.
  - Offered mechanism: cosmic rays → clouds → crops. Dewey had noted no time lag, sometimes human activity slightly *leading* solar change.
- A possible 30–36-year "war/currency" alternation is noted and explicitly rejected as having **too few repetitions to conclude**.

## GSPS relevance
- **Directly reusable methods, both Dewey-compatible:**
  - (a) Symmetric-pair period averaging uses all peaks and down-weights the noisy central interval.
  - (b) Phase by averaging projections from every past extreme. This is a robust form of Gann's own "count from every important top and bottom" instruction (Master Course; Tunnel).
  - GSPS's time-projection code (`timeCycles.ts`, `coordinateLedger.ts`) projects from anchors. A future enhancement could report the **dispersion** of projections from multiple past pivots as a confidence measure, i.e. cluster counts of time projections.
- **Honesty benchmark:** Tomes declines the 30–36-year claim for too few repetitions. That is the Dewey "repetition count" item applied correctly, and the standard GSPS should hold itself to.

## Three-question notes
1. **Gann:** not a source. The method parallels Gann's "count time from every past top and bottom and look where counts cluster."
2. **Dewey checklist:**
   - Addressed: repetition count (10 cycles), regularity of timing (±6 months), constancy of period (5.44–5.61 across pairs), cross-series clustering (the same period in many series).
   - Not addressed: dominance (no spectrum shown), wave-shape identity, phase-resumption after distortion. The controlled-price era is a distortion, but resumption is not tested.
3. **Hermetic:**
   - **Rhythm**: a single dominant pendulum-like period.
   - **Cause and Effect**: sun → clouds → crops → prices is an explicit causal chain.
   - Correspondence only weakly (½ of the sunspot cycle).
