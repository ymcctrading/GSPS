## Structure (what the book is)
- **Foreword (May 9 1927):** threefold purpose (romance, moral lesson and "natural laws laid down in the Bible", foreknowledge). It "contains a valuable secret, clothed in veiled language", found on the second or third reading. The Bible "contains the key to the process by which you may know all there is to know." "Jesus rose on the third day and rested on the seventh day. Robert Gordon's seven days will no longer be a mystery."
- **Ch. I–V, XI–XV, XVII, XX–XXIV:** the life of Robert Gordon (born **Sat Jun 9 1906**, near Texarkana; Gemini, like Gann himself, born Jun 6 1878), his love for Marie Stanton and her disappearance on Jun 5 1927. Also Bible study and poems, a clairvoyant and astrologers, spiritualism at Silver Springs, and Sebring. **No operational market content** in these chapters beyond the items below.
- **Ch. V, XXV–XXXVI:** a prophetic future war in the air (1930–32) against Japan, Spain, Mexico, then England and Europe. Robert's inventions (a four-winged "Ezekiel" plane, a silent muffler, a thought recorder, a death ray, sleeping gas, the "Tunnel thru the Air" vacuum corridor) save the U.S. It ends with a world peace conference in New York on Aug 30 1932, where Marie returns.

## Market and cycle content (the substance for GSPS)
### Ch. VII "Future Cycles" (Jan 28 1927): Gann's cycle credo, in Robert's voice
- "In making my predictions I use **geometry and mathematics**, just as the astronomer does, based on immutable laws."
- "**History repeats itself… in order to know and predict the future of anything you only have to look up what has happened in the past and get a correct base or starting point**" (Eccl 1:9). "If we have the right beginning, we will get the right ending."
- "**Everything in this universe is elliptical or circular in motion… Every thought you think makes a circle, and it comes back to you.**"
- "**The great law of vibration is based on like producing like. Like causes produce like effects.**"
- "**The cycle theory, or harmonic analysis**, is the only thing that we can rely upon to ascertain the future." This is Gann himself naming his method *harmonic analysis*, which is the direct bridge to Tomes (C04).
- "**IT IS NOT MY AIM TO EXPLAIN THE CAUSE OF CYCLES.**"
- "**In every law of nature there is a major and a minor; a positive, a negative, and a neutral. Therefore… a lesser, a greater and intermediate cycle, or cycles within cycles.** Like Ezekiel says: 'Wheel within a wheel.'"
- Time units come from the Earth:
  - **4 minutes = 1° of rotation** ("the smallest cycle… in things that are very active and have a high vibration")
  - 24 hours
  - 1 year (the four seasons)
- Major cycles are **100 and 5,000 years**, plus the **1,000-year** cycle (AD 916–923 ≈ 1914–1923). "Seven years from the close of the World War" gave the 1923–26 troubles (Joseph's 7 years).
- "It is just as easy to figure 100 years or 1000 years in the future as one or two years ahead, if you have the correct starting point and know the cycle."
- Ch. VIII: 100-year repetitions in transport history (the 1819 steamship vs the 1919 R-34, and so on).

### The 1927 trading narrative (Ch. IX–XVI, XIX): the method as dramatised
- **Cotton, Jan–Jun 1927:**
  - Buy July cotton at 13.80 on Jan 24 ("sure… would never decline to 13.25"), margined to 12.80.
  - Profits wiped out by mid-March; hold (the flood was forecast).
  - Apr 20 flood, then up. Buy more on the way up (Apr 30).
  - Grains bought May 19 with stops, sold May 28 ("top around May 28 – June 1").
  - Cotton sold Jun 1; wheat shorted Jun 1 and covered Jun 4; bought again Jun 6–7 ("bottom"); sold Jun 10 ("top for a reaction").
  - These are dates forecast from "the cycle", then traded both ways.
- **Ch. XVI, "Great campaign in cotton" (Jun–Oct 1927):**
  - "last buying level around June 25"; up "about thirty days" to Jul 25; short Jul 25 to Aug 6
  - long for the **Aug 8 Government report**; reverse at the Aug 9 spike
  - pyramid up Aug 13–29 to "final high Sep 5–8" at the September report; short and pyramid down; turn Sep 23
  - tops and bottoms timed around **crop-report dates** and fixed day counts
- **Ch. XVI, "Great campaign in Major Motors" (a stand-in for General Motors, incorporated Sep 16 1908):**
  - Jun 19 1927: "a **new and great discovery of a time factor**": decline to about Jun 30, then advance **"until about September 16th, 1927, when the Company would be 19 years old"**, to about 270, then down into Feb–Apr 1929.
  - "**His great discovery of what stocks would do at a certain age**." ⇒ **the incorporation-date anniversary as a time anchor**, corroborating the Master Course's incorporation-date calendar (Ch. 7).
  - Covered shorts Jun 30 at 192½ and went long. Raised the stop; stopped out Aug 8 at 225. Re-bought at 218 after the "12 to 15 point" reaction he expected, stop 212 ("a point… it would not decline to"). Sold 2,200 shares at 272 on the Sep 16 target, then shorted the new stock with a stop 10 points above.
  - **Pyramiding rule, verbatim substance:** "**buy or sell the largest amount first and then gradually decrease… on the way up or down, and always using a stop loss order.**" A decreasing pyramid (cf. the Overnight Chart's halving pyramid, Master Course).
  - **Money-management rule:** "after he had made a large amount of profits… **never risk more than 10 per cent of his profits on the first new deal**, and if that deal went wrong… decrease his trading so that the next loss would only be 10 per cent of his remaining profits… the market would have to beat him ten consecutive times." ⇒ Gann's 10% rule stated as **risk as a percentage of current equity, shrinking after losses** (cf. `lib/risk/*` ceilings and the 10%-of-capital ceiling in A3/A5).
- **Ch. XVI, old Henry Watson's lessons from the ruined operators (Drew, Sully, Livermore, Scales, Ryan, Durant, Lawson):**
  - **Over-diversification** ("too many irons in the fire… get out of stocks… on which they were making money and keep those that were going against them").
  - **Not stopping when the tide turns.**
  - **Losing judgment at the height of prosperity.**
  - "**Man's greatest enemy in speculation is 'hope.'** He refuses to face facts." Harriman "stuck to one class… his manipulations… **conform closely to the law of harmonic analysis**."
  - Robert "**never scattered over two or three markets at the same time**" (Ch. XIX).
  - Robert: "**There is a time and a season for everything**… a time to stop trying to make money and to keep what you have, then wait for another season."
- **Ch. XIX:** covers shorts before Christmas 1927 "on the big decline… the public had lost confidence."

### Bible-derived time arithmetic (Ch. XVII, XXXI)
- Daniel 12:12's **1,335 days** gives "troublesome times" **March 1931 → end of June 1932**. The 1,290 vs 1,335 days differ by **45 days**.
- "**A time… meant 360 days, 360 years, or 360 degrees**… half a time meant **180**"; "**a day for a year**" (Ezek 4:6). ⇒ Gann's own statement of the **1° = 1 day = 1 year** equivalence behind his wheel counts.
- **7 × 7 = 49-year jubilee** periods:
  - NYC evacuated Nov 25 1783 + 3 × 49 → 1930
  - NYC founded 1614 + 6 × 49 → 1908, + 24½ (½ of 49) → 1932
  - Roanoke 1585 + 343 (7 × 49) → 1928
  - 1914 + 14 years → 1928, + **42 months (½ of 7 years)** → the war's length
  - "seventy times seven" = 490 years from 1492 → 1982 (end of wars)
- **Presidential forecast by initial letters (Ch. XVIII)**: letter-repetition numerology. ⚠ **No authorized specification; not admissible even as confluence** under AGENTS.md (no rule, no method, just name letters).

### The veiled 1929–32 map (why readers credited the novel with forecasts)
The fictional war runs on the calendar the market actually followed:
- troubles 1928–32
- the fall of Chicago and **the NYSE closing in panic on Oct 2–3 1931**, against the real **Dow low of Oct 5 1931** (see A11: a reader credited *Tunnel* with the Oct 3 1931 bottom)
- the decisive battles **Jun 8–9 and Jul 4 1932** (Robert's birthday and Independence Day), against the **real Dow bottom of Jul 8 1932**
- peace Aug 4 / Aug 30 1932, against the strong Jul–Sep 1932 rally

⇒ The "secret" is at least partly a **dated forecast of the 1929–32 cycle hidden in the plot**. The method that produced it is not disclosed.

### Hermetic and philosophical content in the text (Gann's own framing)
- "**All the laws of Nature taught the eternal law of action and reaction.** For every day of sunshine comes a night of darkness" (Ch. XXIII): Polarity and Rhythm in Gann's words.
- "Whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap"; Emerson's *Compensation* (Cause and Effect). "In silence, by silence, through silence were all things made." "The kingdom of heaven is within you" (Mentalism).
- Kennelworth: "**three great emotions: Love, Fear and Hate**, which actuate every deed" (cf. A11's hope/fear mass-pressure cycle).
- Astrology: Robert "believe[s] in the stars… the planets rule our destinies." Horoscopes appear in the plot (Venus/Mars/Uranus transits). **Narrative only; no technique is specified.**

## Three-question notes
1. Tier A (public book, 1927), fiction. Cite *statements of method* (Ch. VII, XVI) directly. Treat plot "forecasts" as evidence of Gann's beliefs and of his 1927 dated expectations, not as rules.
2. Dewey: Gann explicitly declines to explain causes ("not my aim"). His 1,000- and 100-year analogies are anecdotal matching, which fails **repetition count** and **dominance**. The book's value is as the source of the **"harmonic analysis" framing**, which Tomes later formalises (Part C).
3. Hermetic: **Rhythm** (action and reaction, cycles within cycles), **Polarity** (positive, negative, neutral; sunshine and night), **Correspondence** ("wheel within a wheel", 1° = 1 day = 1 year), **Cause and Effect** ("like causes produce like effects"), **Mentalism** ("every thought… makes a circle", "the kingdom of heaven is within you"), **Vibration** ("the great law of vibration"). **Gender** is least present, apart from the "positive/negative/neutral" triad.
