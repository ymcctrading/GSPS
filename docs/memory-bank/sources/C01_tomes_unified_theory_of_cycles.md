## Content (synthesis)
- **The empirical start (1977).** Twenty-two New Zealand economic series showed regular cycles of **4.45, 5.9, 7.15 and 9.0 years**. These are integer fractions of about **35.7 years** (÷8, ÷6, ÷5, ÷4). Tomes later added 3.0 (×12) and 4.0 (×9).
  - Hirst and Dewey (1967), using different data, reached **17.7–17.9 years**, which is half of that figure.
  - The ultimate "fundamental" is longer still. Both 36 and the 54-year Kondratieff are fractions of **108 years**.
- **Dewey 1967, "Cycle Frequency Harmonics."** Repeatedly ×/÷ 2 and 3 from **17.75 years** generates most common cycles. Ratios of 5 and 7 appear only occasionally.
- **Musical structure.**
  - Daily corn prices over 44 years show cycle frequencies arranged like the white notes of a piano.
  - The 4:5:6:8 ratios of the original economic cycles form a **major chord**.
  - D. S. Castle (1956) found stock-market cycles fit the musical scale over three octaves, with a fundamental of 36.5 or 54.7 years.
- **Dewey 1970, "Cycle Synchrony."** Cycles of the same period in unrelated series have **clustered phases**. Dewey said this was what removed his remaining doubt that cycles are real.
- **Mechanism, the paper's core contribution.**
  - Real cause→effect relationships are **non-linear response functions**, often with lags.
  - A sine wave fed through a non-linear response function yields **only integer harmonics** of the input.
  - The first harmonic stays **in phase (or 180° out of phase)** with the input, so synchrony survives multi-step causal chains. This explains Dewey's synchrony observation.
  - Lagged (linear) functions create no new frequencies; they only change amplitude and phase.
  - Multiple inputs can create sum and difference frequencies.
- **Harmonic power.** In a complex network, harmonic H can be reached through as many paths as there are ordered factorisations of H.
  - Examples: 5 → 1 way; 6 → 3; 24 → 20; 576 → 1,376.
  - With roughly 1/frequency fading, this gives a structured power spectrum. In the octave 48–96, the strongest harmonics are 48, 54, 60, 64, 72, 80, 84, 90, 96, which is a musical scale (C D E F G A B♭ B C).
- **Planetary section (astronomy; recorded but not admissible for GSPS; see "Astrology" in AGENTS.md).**
  - Tidal, centre-of-mass and gravitational models are compared. Tomes proposes a relativistic photon-displacement mechanism.
  - Only force oscillations near the Sun's 25–30-day rotation build up.
  - Modulation periods: 3.45, 3.95, 5.9, 8.6, 11.1 and 19 years.
- **"Average cycle vs specific occurrence."** This is the paper's most transferable methodological point.
  - J-V-E alignments recur on **average** every **3.446** and **11.07** years. The intervals actually observed are **3.244 / 4.542** and **10.38 / 12.00** years, in roughly a 4:3 mix.
  - The same applies to the outer planets. The J-S-U-N average is **171.4** years, while **178.7** is only the most common specific occurrence. Researchers who took 178.7 as the period were wrong.
  - ⇒ A cycle can be real on average while no single interval equals the average. Phase must be tracked across many repetitions, not read off one interval.
- **G. T. Lane (Cycles, 1950):** the **41-month** stock cycle is *phase-modulated* by a **22-year** cycle.
- **Table 4, "times-two" family:** 1.12, 2.25, 4.5, 9, **18** (Dewey/Hirst fundamental), **36** (Tomes), 72, **144**, **288**, 575, 1150, 2300, 4600 years. Tomes says this is the dominant family but not the only one; 22.5 and 10.0 also exist.
- **The author's 2005 note** says this paper's version of the calculation wrongly makes dominant cycles always related by 2. The corrected theory, C04, gives about one ratio of 3 per two to three ratios of 2.

## Three-question notes
1. **Gann:** not a Gann source. It independently rationalises Gann's ½/⅓/¼ divisions and his 9/36/72/144 numbers as the strongest harmonics of a 2×3 lattice. That is a mechanism claim by Tomes, not a Gann citation.
2. **Dewey checklist:**
   - Addresses **cross-series clustering** (synchrony) and **constancy of period**, including the average-vs-specific caution.
   - Offers a physical story for **phase-resumption**: harmonics stay in phase with their driver.
   - No significance testing; the power spectrum is theoretical.
3. **Hermetic:**
   - **Vibration** (everything as waves and harmonics) and **Correspondence** (the same ratio structure from days to millennia, "as above, so below") are the direct fits.
   - **Cause and Effect**: the paper's defining move is to explain *why* ratios appear, via response functions.
   - Rhythm is implied.
   - Not Polarity, Gender or Mentalism: nothing here concerns opposites, generation or mind.
