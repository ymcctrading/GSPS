## *Economic Forecaster* (1954) — Drive `661617074-WD-Gann-1954-Economic-Forecaster.pdf`, 10 PDF pages

- **Length.** PDF 1 is the back and front cover; PDF 2–10 are two-page spreads. The booklet is **16 printed pages plus covers**, so the catalog's "16-page booklet" is right. A11's "8 pp." counted PDF sheets of a different scan.
- **Every page OCR'd and read**, including the testimonial columns that A11 calls garbled.
- **New against A11** (paraphrased):
  - **"Why Time Cycles Predict Trend of Commodities, Stocks and Business"**, signed **April 5, 1954**.
    - Cycles repeat because human nature doesn't change.
    - Over-borrowing on hope in prosperity is liquidated in fear.
  - **"Opportunities for Profits".** The greatest opportunities come at the end of great time cycles, when moves are fast.
    - Courses for trade buyers use the Master Charts, the Master Calculators and the Master Three-Dimension Chart.
    - "W. D. Gann's greatest discoveries on Time Cycles have come from the Bible."
  - **The Master Calculator brochure.**
    - **"We now have two Master Calculators … Numbers 9 and 12."**
    - It "proves 9 divisions of time from one center of time factor" and locates "the Corner Stone, the Key Stone and the Cap Stone" for price and time.
    - Credited forecasts: the soybean advance from Aug 1953 to Mar 1954, and coffee's 1954 record high.
    - A cotton trader quotes a call of top for March cotton at 40.20 on Sept 8, 1952 and a low at 32.10 on Jan 12, 1953 (actual: 32.12 that day).
    - It is sold only with the Master Courses.
  - **The course list and prices (1954).**
    - Stocks: Course No. 1, *Speculation a Profitable Profession*, $200, with 18 charts and **"one new chart, the Dollar Trend, which is much more accurate than the Dow-Jones Averages"** (this matches the Master Course Ch. 1 plates of NYSE total market value). Course No. 2, the Master Forecasting Course on Stocks, $7,500.
    - Commodities: Course No. 1, $200 (with personal instruction $300); Course No. 2, New Time Trend Indicator, $600; Course No. 3, $200 per commodity; the Master Forecasting Course on Grains, Cotton, Coffee, Lard and Eggs, $1,500 ($2,000 with personal instruction), including two Master Calculators.
    - The **Great Master Course**, $5,000: the Master Time Factor, the Great Time Cycles and Master Charts.
    - **"With all Master Courses we send you a mass-pressure chart calculated for one year in the future."**
  - **A CPA letter of May 1954** (J. Zittrer) vouching for the booklet's historical record.
- **What it adds to the build:** nothing new to implement. It names the 1954 tools (Master Calculators 9 and 12, the Three-Dimension Chart, the Dollar Trend, the mass-pressure chart) without disclosing them. The Dollar Trend corroborates the market-value gauge in Master Course Ch. 1.
