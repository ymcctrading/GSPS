# Gann historical source materials — memory bank

Five primary/secondary documents on W.D. Gann's method were reviewed in a
2026-09-15 research session and are indexed here so a future session can
work from this summary instead of re-uploading the source PDFs. Only the
first two are Gann's own words and are public domain (pre-1928 US
publication); the other three are third-party interpretive or promotional
material of varying reliability and are **not** reproduced here beyond
short, clearly-marked quotes — summarized and assessed instead.

Organizing note: the seven Hermetic principles (Mentalism, Correspondence,
Vibration, Polarity, Rhythm, Cause and Effect, Gender — the framework
popularized in *The Kybalion* and carried forward in Dennis William Hauck's
alchemical writing) are used below as a classification lens, at the
project owner's request, because Gann's own 1909 vocabulary ("Law of
Vibration," cyclic "harmony," universal "causation") is drawn from the same
early-1900s esoteric register as the Hermetic tradition — this is a real
throughline in the source material, not an overlay invented for this
document. The lens organizes; it does not substitute for the evidentiary
weight each source is given.

## 1. The 1909 Ticker interview — *Correspondence* & *Cause and Effect*

- **Source:** "William D. Gann: An Operator Whose Science and Ability Place
  Him in the Front Rank," *The Ticker and Investment Digest*, Dec. 1909
  (R.D. Wyckoff, editor/publisher). Public domain.
- **In repo:** `docs/doctrine/TheTickerandInvestmentDigest_gannmastery.com.pdf`.
- **Content:** the origin-story/credibility piece. Introduces the "Law of
  Vibration" — markets move by a natural, periodic law, the same category
  as wireless telegraphy; each stock has its own individual "rate of
  vibration" (Correspondence: as the physical law governs radio waves, so
  it governs price). States 10 years of preparatory study, 9 months at the
  Astor Library/British Museum studying records to 1820, and a documented
  track record (286 trades in October 1909, 264 profitable). Explicitly
  affirms "every effect must have an adequate cause" (Cause and Effect) —
  and explicitly **withholds** the method: "Mr. Gann has refused to
  disclose his method at any price."
- **What's notably absent:** no Square of 9, no Gann angles, no
  numerology, no astrology. This is a claims-and-credibility piece, not a
  how-to.
- **Reliability:** primary source, highest confidence for what it actually
  claims; the claims themselves (track record, prediction accuracy) are
  self-reported and testimonial, not independently audited.

## 2. *Truth of the Stock Tape* (1923) — *Rhythm* & *Polarity*

- **Source:** W.D. Gann, *Truth of the Stock Tape: A Study of the Stock and
  Commodity Markets*, Financial Guardian Publishing Co., 1923. Public
  domain (Internet Archive `in.ernet.dli.2015.166248`, same scan the
  project owner linked from Wikimedia Commons). Full OCR text pulled from
  `jacquan801/gann-cycle-research` (GitHub, public repo) since direct
  upload/URL fetch was blocked by this session's egress policy.
- **Content — four books in one, overwhelmingly a disclosed risk-management
  and tape-reading manual, not a geometric-forecasting manual:**
  - **Money management** (12 numbered rules): capital sized to share price
    tier; mandatory stop-loss, never more than 3–5 points risked; the three
    fatal mistakes named as overtrading, no stop, and averaging a loss;
    move stop to breakeven once ahead; never fix a target price in advance;
    pyramid only into winners (never scale into losers); diversify across
    groups; never trade on dividends alone.
  - **Operating method:** short-selling given full parity with buying
    ("be a Bear in a Bear market and a Bull in a Bull market" — Polarity);
    no hedging across groups; no scale-in averaging.
  - **Seven Zones of Activity:** a market-phase model — Normal, three zones
    above (quiet advance → increasing activity → wild distribution), three
    below (quiet decline → increasing liquidation → panic/accumulation),
    then back to Normal — a cyclic Rhythm, psychological/volume-described
    rather than formula-described.
  - **Habits of Stocks / Crossing Old Levels:** the closest thing to a
    mechanical rule in the book — buy/sell near old tops/bottoms with a
    stop 3 points beyond; a level crossed a *second* time is safer than the
    first crossing; worked case studies with real prices/dates (Republic
    Steel, Crucible Steel, U.S. Steel, Wabash Pfd A, United Retail).
  - **Commodities (Book IV):** same rules transplanted to cotton/wheat/corn.
- **What's still withheld, even here:** "The most important thing of all is
  the Time factor, which I use in making up my annual forecasts. It is not
  my object here to give away that secret." Same gap as 1909.
- **Reliability:** primary source, highest confidence, and the only one of
  the five with concrete, falsifiable, dated case studies.

## 3. Awodele, *W.D. Gann: Divination by Mathematics II — Harmonic Analysis* (2013) — *Vibration*

- **Source:** self-published (BEKH LLC, Union KY), copyright 2013, all
  rights reserved. **Not** a Gann text — a modern researcher's
  investigation of a single passage in Gann's 1927 novel *The Tunnel Thru
  the Air*, where a character claims E.H. Harriman used a method
  conforming to "the law of harmonic analysis."
- **Content:** the author identifies "harmonic analysis" as the real
  mathematical technique — Fourier decomposition of a time series into
  dominant periodic cycles — and demonstrates it step-by-step on Henry
  Ludwell Moore's 1914 rainfall-cycle book (public domain) and on stock
  price data (CSX weekly, finding a 106-week dominant cycle and using it
  to project a curve forward). This part is legitimate, correctly-described
  signal processing.
  The strongest piece of *primary-source corroboration* in the whole book:
  a 1922 newspaper article says Gann's method was "simplified from that
  propounded by Henry Ludwell Moore," and a 1926 Gann letter to a client
  states "I have read Professor Moore's book... you will find Schuster and
  Fourier theories helpful in analyzing the market." That is real evidence
  Gann's actual mathematical basis leaned on cycle/spectral analysis, not
  astrology or numerology alone.
  The book's final two chapters ("Additional Confirmation," "The Cause of
  Cycles") then speculate — planetary-orbit numerology, Pythagorean magic
  squares, 17th–19th century occult texts — reasoning the author himself
  frames as unresolved, not established.
- **Reliability:** secondary, mixed — the Fourier-analysis method itself is
  sound and implementable; the Gann-attribution evidence is real but thin
  (one letter, one article); the "cause of cycles" chapters are openly
  speculative and should not be treated as Gann doctrine.

## 4. Numerology forum fragment ("WD Gann Number Vibrations") — *Vibration*, cautionary

- **Source:** not a book — a 3-page mailing-list/forum exchange (a poster
  "Brett" replying to "David," likely from the Gann Study Group Yahoo
  group), saved as a PDF. No author credentials, no editorial vetting.
- **Content:** digital-root reduction (sum digits repeatedly to get 1–9,
  e.g. 144 → 9) applied to the 360° circle (30°-step multiples reduce to a
  repeating 3-6-9 cycle) and to a price-percentage table (multiples of
  3.125% = 100/32); multiples of 144 (Fibonacci/Square-of-9-adjacent) all
  reduce to 9. Cites a real early-1900s numerology author, L. Dow Balliett,
  as a plausible source of Gann's "vibration" vocabulary.
- **Caution flagged in this session:** digital-root reduction is casting
  out nines — arithmetic mod 9. Any arithmetic sequence with a common
  difference produces a cyclic digital-root pattern *by construction*; this
  is true of any multiple-of-30 or multiple-of-9 sequence regardless of
  whether it has anything to do with markets. The "144 always reduces to 9"
  observation is true of every multiple of 9 — not a discovery about that
  specific number. Treat as a weak, unverified fragment; the Balliett
  etymology lead is the one genuinely useful thread.
- **Reliability:** lowest of the five — no author, no evidence beyond
  "go back-test it yourself."

## 5. WD Gann Inc. company blog + "D Levels" table — *Gender* (active/receptive market forces) & raw geometry

- **Source A ("Mathematical Seer"):** a captured printout of
  `blog.wdgann.com` (05/08/2015), the blog of the company holding Gann's
  copyrights/archives (Lambert-Gann Publishing / WD Gann Inc.). Mostly
  promotional (chart packages, book-edition collector notes, GATE
  seasonality software from an unrelated third party), but contains two
  primary-source-adjacent items:
  - A reprinted **public-domain 1923 newspaper article** describing Gann's
    **letter/name numerology method for election forecasting** — candidate
    names' starting/ending letters, "fortunate numbers" (2, 4, 6, 8, 11)
    tied to birthdate and calendar dates (July 4, inauguration day). A
    distinct technique from anything in the 1909/1923 books, applied to
    non-market predictions.
  - **Direct confirmation, from the company holding his actual working
    papers, that Gann used real astrology**: his private planetary
    ephemeris (working diary) is described as annotated with "planetary
    midpoints, declinations, Heliocentric transits, and the Lunar Nodes"
    for nearly every month, and a specific trade (short soybeans, Dec.
    1948) tied to a Jupiter/Mars conjunction. This is the strongest
    evidence across all five sources that astrology was a genuine working
    technique, not just later writers' projection — though the framing
    throughout is commercial (a company selling reproductions/ephemeris
    copies).
- **Source B ("D Levels"):** not a book — a bare 15-page spreadsheet, no
  prose. For each integer *n*, gives *n²* and seven values interpolated at
  45° steps to *(n+1)²* — i.e. the classic **Square of 9 spiral mechanism**
  in raw lookup-table form (walking 45° around the spiral from a root
  converts angular position to a price level). This is the same geometry
  `lib/gann/squareOf9.ts` already implements algorithmically in this
  codebase — the table is decodable, uncredited, but mathematically
  unambiguous.
- **Reliability:** Source A's numerology/astrology claims are real
  historical artifacts (a real newspaper reprint, a real company
  describing real archival material) but promotional in framing; Source B
  is a pure, verifiable geometric fact table, effectively self-authenticating.
