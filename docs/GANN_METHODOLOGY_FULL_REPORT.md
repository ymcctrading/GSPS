# Part I — The Method

## 1. Who Gann was, and why the mystique is load-bearing, not incidental

William Delbert Gann (1878–1955) built a forty-five-year public career on a
single repeated pattern: publish extensive, genuinely useful trading
discipline; state, in each and nearly every one of ten books over that
period, that this discipline rests on a deeper mathematical/geometric/
temporal method; and then explicitly, deliberately withhold that method's
actual mechanism from the reading public. This is not a modern researcher's
inference — it is Gann's own repeated, first-person statement, made across
a 1909 magazine profile, a 1923 book, a 1927 novel, and a 1949 capstone
work spanning his entire career (`GANN_HISTORICAL_SOURCES.md` A1, A2, A3,
A9).

The 1909 profile (A1, *The Ticker and Investment Digest*, written by R.D.
Wyckoff — later famous in his own right as a market technician) is the
origin document for nearly everything that follows. It reports ten years of
preparatory study, nine months spent at the Astor Library and British
Museum studying market records back to 1820, and a claimed, dated track
record: 286 trades in October 1909, 264 of them profitable. It introduces
the phrase that anchors Gann's entire vocabulary for the next four decades —
the **"Law of Vibration"**, the claim that every stock has its own
individual "rate of vibration," explicitly compared to the physics of
wireless telegraphy, then a genuinely new technology. And it states, in so
many words, that Gann refused to disclose the method behind this claim "at
any price." No Square of Nine. No angles. No numerology. No astrology. A
claims-and-credibility piece, not a how-to — and, read against everything
that came after it, the first instance of a pattern that never really
changed: sell discipline and track record, withhold mechanism.

By 1927's *Tunnel Thru the Air* (A3) — a coded science-fiction novel whose
final two chapters drop the fiction entirely and speak in Gann's own voice,
dated "January 28, 1927" — the withholding is explicit and capitalized:
*"I have always looked for causes and when once I determine a cause I can
always be sure of the effect... IT IS NOT MY AIM TO EXPLAIN THE CAUSE OF
CYCLES. The general public is not yet ready for it."* The same book names
the operative technique directly for the only time in any source reviewed —
*"the cycle theory, or harmonic analysis, is the only thing that we can rely
upon to ascertain the future"* — and grounds the whole enterprise
explicitly in Scripture: *"I hold that the Bible contains the key to the
process by which man may know all there is to know of the future,"* with
Matthew 7:7 cited as method-instruction and Ecclesiastes 1:9 ("there is
nothing new under the sun") as the warrant for "history repeats." This is
not incidental religious color. Gann's 1950 devotional concordance *The
Magic Word* (A10) and his 1940 political pamphlet *Face Facts America!*
(A7) both confirm the same worldview held to the end of his life: markets,
history, and Scripture are read through the same lens, because to Gann they
are expressions of the same underlying law. A7 states this bridge outright:
*"By the use of these Master Time cycles... I can forecast important
culminating periods in the history of countries or wars just the same as I
forecast important tops and bottoms in the stock market."*

By 1949's *45 Years in Wall Street* (A9), his capstone book written five
years before his death, nothing about the pattern had changed. A specific,
dated 1950–1953 forecast is given, attributed to his "Master Time Cycle" —
with, in Gann's own words elsewhere in the same book's tradition, zero
mechanism disclosed. Forty years and ten books after the 1909 profile, the
same structure holds: real, useful, generously detailed trading discipline
in print; the actual timing mechanism kept out of every book he ever sold
at a bookstore.

**This is the single most important fact for interpreting everything that
follows, including for a platform trying to reconstruct or extend the
method.** Gann ran two curricula simultaneously for his entire career: a
public one (ten books, decades of subscriber bulletins) built almost
entirely on money management, tape reading, and a handful of arithmetic
price rules; and a private, expensive one (a paid correspondence course
priced, per his own 1954 promotional booklet A11, from $200 to $5,000 —
a genuinely large sum for the era) that added geometry (Square of Nine,
Gann angles) and, more sparingly, astrology. Nothing in the public books
ever crosses into the private curriculum's content. This split is not
inferred from silence — it is directly evidenced by the discovery, in this
research pass, of an actual transcription of that private course (A2.1)
and a signed 1954 private client letter (A2.3), both of which contain, in
Gann's own voice, techniques absent from every one of his public books.
Anyone reconstructing "the Gann method" from only the public books —
which is what most popular trading literature, and GSPS's own prior
implementation passes, have effectively done — is reconstructing one half
of a two-tier system and calling it the whole.

## 2. Layer 1 — Discipline (the fully disclosed majority of the actual content)

Strip away the mystique, and what Gann actually put in print, repeatedly
and in overwhelming volume relative to everything else, is a risk-management
and trading-discipline system. This is not a minor precursor to "the real
method" — across five books (*Truth of the Stock Tape* 1923, *Wall Street
Stock Selector* 1930, *New Stock Trend Detector* 1936, *How to Make Profits
Trading in Commodities* 1941/51, *45 Years in Wall Street* 1949) it is the
majority of the actual page count, and it is the one layer with a genuinely
unbroken, forty-five-year paper trail of direct disclosure.

### 2.1 The Never-Failing Rules, unified across their four published versions

Gann published an expanding, self-referential version of the same core
rule list four separate times, each version citing and building on the
last:

- **1923** (*Truth of the Stock Tape*, A2): twelve numbered rules — the
  seed list. Mandatory stops. Never average a loser. Pyramid winners only,
  never losers. Never fix a profit target in advance and hold past a trend
  change hoping to reach it.
- **1930** (*Wall Street Stock Selector*, A4): **24** "Never-failing" rules —
  the twelve expanded with joint-account handling, odd-lot rounding,
  even-figure price avoidance (place orders ½–¾ point off round numbers,
  since public and stop orders cluster exactly there), and personal-luck/
  trend tracking.
- **1941/51** (*How to Make Profits Trading in Commodities*, A8): **28**
  "Valuable" rules, verbatim in the recovered portion of the text — capital
  divided into ten parts, risk never more than one-tenth per trade;
  mandatory stops sized 1–3¢ (max 5¢); never average a loss; never hedge;
  reduce size after a loss, never increase it; explicitly "avoid double
  mistakes" (a wrong entry compounded by a wrong exit).
- **1949** (*45 Years in Wall Street*, A9): the **24** Never-Failing Rules,
  printed for the first time as a single bound, numbered list — the
  definitive, most-often-cited consolidation of everything above, distilled
  from four decades of the same underlying discipline. No materially new
  rule versus 1930/1941; this is the canonical citation version.

The rule that recurs across every single one of these four lists, stated
independently in at least three different books and once more inside the
fictional frame of *Tunnel Thru the Air* (A3) as a specific pyramiding
example: **never risk more than roughly 10% of trading capital on any one
position**, sized so that ten consecutive losing trades — an explicitly
worst-case, not a typical, scenario — would be needed to exhaust the
account. This figure recurring independently across a public book (A5), a
coded novel (A3), and a commodities manual (A8) is the strongest evidence
in the whole corpus that this specific number was a genuine, long-held
personal rule of Gann's, not a rhetorical flourish invented for one
audience.

### 2.2 The Rule of Three

Introduced in 1930 (A4) as, in Gann's own account, a rule traders paid him
$1,000 for: **a stock in a confirmed uptrend will not close three
consecutive days lower without signaling at least a temporary reversal**;
the mirror rule for downtrends requires two consecutive higher closes.
Worked in the book against U.S. Steel's actual 1929 daily closes. This is a
genuinely distinct, narrow, mechanically simple rule — not a restatement of
old-level crossing, and not (see §17 below) the same thing as Sara Sniper
Strat's bar-shape classification, even though both count consecutive bars.

### 2.3 The "boiling point" and duration heuristics

Also from 1930: final blow-off advances or declines in active stocks
typically exhaust in six to seven weeks, rarely running past ten. A
specific, testable duration claim, distinct from the longer duration-based
"time square" bands in §5 below (which run from single-digit days to
multi-year windows) — this one is narrowly scoped to blow-off/climax
moves specifically.

### 2.4 Chart-timeframe hierarchy and the "power ratio"

1930's *Wall Street Stock Selector* states an explicit hierarchy across
daily/weekly/monthly charts, with a stated "power ratio" — weekly moves
carry roughly seven times the significance of daily moves, monthly roughly
thirty times, yearly roughly 365 times — and the general claim that longer
accumulation periods produce proportionally bigger subsequent moves. 1941's
commodities book (A8) adds a **Quarterly Chart** as a fourth timeframe used
the same way. This is the direct ancestor of GSPS's own multi-timeframe
philosophy (`docs/GANN_SARA_CONFLUENCE.md`'s decision hierarchy requiring
"valid market regime" before a Gann/Sara read matters at all), though GSPS
does not implement Gann's specific numeric power-ratio weighting between
timeframes anywhere today.

### 2.5 "Sections of a campaign," and Major vs. Minor trend

A bull or bear market normally runs three to four legs ("sections"), with
later sections — the third and fourth — weighted more heavily than the
second for trend-change confirmation (A5, A8). *45 Years in Wall Street*
sharpens this into a formalized **Major vs. Minor trend** distinction: trade
minor-trend reversals only at a rule-defined top or bottom with a tight
stop, and keep that separate from major-trend positioning. This concept
partially overlaps GSPS's own `lib/gann/swingChart.ts` construction (see
§4.7 below) but is not a literal port of it.

### 2.6 Capital sizing, worked in real numbers

*How to Make Profits Trading in Commodities* (A8) gives a concrete capital
table behind the "divide capital into ten parts" rule: roughly $3,000 of
capital per 10,000-bushel wheat position with a stop no wider than 3¢;
roughly $300 per 1,000-bushel "job lot," same stop; scaled figures for
wheat priced $0.40–$0.60 and $1.75–$2.50. *New Stock Trend Detector* (A5)
gives the equity-side equivalent: roughly $3,000 capital per 100-share lot,
never more than 10% of capital at risk, a maximum 5-point initial risk, and
price-band velocity banding (stocks move faster above $50, faster still
above $100). None of this is a Gann-specific technique in the geometric
sense — it is disciplined, era-appropriate position sizing — but it is
useful evidence for exactly how conservative Gann's own real-money practice
actually was, relative to the mystique.

### 2.7 Puts, calls, and warrants

*How to Make Profits Trading in Puts and Calls* (1937/41, A6) and Chapter
XII of *45 Years in Wall Street* (A9) extend the same discipline to a
market structure very different from modern listed options: bespoke,
dealer-written, NYSE-member-endorsed contracts with fixed schedule-based
premiums, no continuous bid/ask, no secondary market. The techniques
described — buying calls/puts for capped-risk leverage, using puts as a
gap-risk stop-loss substitute, a "trading against the contract" scalping
technique (shorting stock against a still-open call once price stalls at
the strike), rolling/pyramiding monthly rather than using a single stop —
are instrument mechanics and money management layered on top of the timing
rules already disclosed elsewhere in his books, not new entry logic. A6
explicitly cites *Truth of the Stock Tape*, *Wall Street Stock Selector*,
and *New Stock Trend Detector* by name as the source of the underlying
timing.

## 3. Layer 2 — Structure and price geometry

This is the layer most people mean when they say "the Gann method," and it
splits cleanly into two evidentiary tiers that this report treats as
distinct throughout: techniques Gann actually put in a book sold at a
bookstore, and techniques he taught only to paying private-course students.
Getting this distinction right matters enormously for both historical
accuracy and for the licensing/IP argument in Part III — because the
private-tier material is exactly the material that is rare, hard to obtain,
and defensible as proprietary.

### 3.1 Old-level crossing and resistance-level logic — the public tier's crown jewel

This is, by a wide margin, the most repeated, most concretely
worked-example-backed technique across Gann's entire public output. It
appears with real numbers in five of his ten books:

- **1923** (A2): "Crossing Old Levels" — buy or sell near old tops/bottoms
  with a stop 3 points beyond the level; a level crossed a *second* time is
  read as safer than the first crossing. Worked against real 1914–1922 case
  studies (Republic Steel, Crucible Steel, U.S. Steel, Wabash Pfd A, United
  Retail).
- **1930** (A4): implicit in the Rule of Three and the general trend-change
  logic.
- **1936** (A5): the **"3-point rule"**, a sharper, explicitly numeric
  version of the 1923 idea — a break of an old top or bottom must clear it
  by a full 3 points to be trusted, with the stop placed 3 points beyond the
  level. Paired with a 3-day confirmation rule and a 2–3 month
  reaction-duration rule.
- **1941/51** (A8): the fullest treatment. The **Resistance Level method** —
  take a swing's high/low range, divide it by eighths (12.5% steps) and by
  thirds; the 50% "gravity center" is named the single most important
  level, then 75%. The **"lost motion"** concept explains *why* the 3¢/3-point
  stop buffer exists: price typically overshoots a level by up to roughly
  1⅞¢ but rarely a full 3¢, so the buffer is sized to survive normal
  overshoot without being so wide it defeats the stop's purpose. A second
  extraction pass added finer mechanics: 5/8 and 3/8-point rules for narrow
  oscillation around the halfway point, and a "resistance points near same
  levels" technique — when two nearby resistance levels cluster, average
  them into a single combined support/resistance point.
- **1949** (A9): a **refined percentage-resistance hierarchy** with explicit
  importance ranking — **50% (most important) > 100% > 25% > 12.5% > 6.25%
  (extremes only) > 33⅓%/66⅔%** — sharper than 1941's looser eighths/thirds
  treatment, with a worked real-market example: the actual 1929 top landing
  simultaneously on 1250% of the 1896 low and 225% of the 1919 high (multiple
  independent resistance calculations converging on the same real price —
  the confluence principle, see §6, demonstrated on an actual historic top).
  A9 also adds the **"4th time at a level" refinement**: the fourth test of
  the same double/triple top or bottom is markedly less safe than the first
  three — "it nearly always goes through" — and **"Market Over-Balanced,"**
  a price-based analogue to the time-overbalance idea in §5: the first rally
  or decline that exceeds the previous rally or decline *in points* (not
  just duration) signals a trend change.

The through-line across all five books: a swing's high/low range is the
fundamental unit; specific fractions of that range (halves, thirds, eighths)
generate candidate price levels; a level that has already been tested
multiple times, then finally breaks by a specific, disclosed numeric buffer
justified by a specific, disclosed mechanism ("lost motion"), is the
strongest structural signal Gann ever put in print. This is also, not
coincidentally, the one technique GSPS's own backtests have independently
found to be its single most consistently positive-evidence scored
criterion (`historicalSR` — see Part III, §14).

### 3.2 The Square of Nine

**Absent from every one of Gann's ten public books** — a negative finding
confirmed directly by full-text search of readable portions of all ten,
including an explicit statement inside *New Stock Trend Detector* (1936,
A5) itself listing "zero mentions of Square of Nine, Gann angles, 45-degree
geometry, or 'vibration'" anywhere in that book's readable text.

**Directly disclosed by Gann himself, in his own voice, in his private
correspondence course** (A2.1, a 2004 hobbyist transcription of course
material Gann sold roughly 1931–1954, assessed at high confidence as
genuine Gann content). A dedicated chapter describes the construction
directly: numbers spiral outward from a center "1"; numbers falling on the
45°/90°/180° radial lines are named resistance points; "Six Squares of
Nine" (each covering 81 numbers) are described as permanent reference charts
Gann sent to students. The circle's 360° is tied explicitly to the number
9: *"If we divide 360° by 9, we get 40, which measures 40°, 40 months, 40
days, or 40 weeks."* A2.1 also describes a second, parallel geometric
framework alongside it — the **"Master '12' Chart"** (Square of Twelve),
built around the numbers 144/288/432 and their multiples.

Two secondary sources independently corroborate the construction in enough
mechanical detail to serve as a checkable reference: the WD Gann Inc.
company blog (B3, from the company holding Gann's actual copyrights and
archives) includes a bare "D Levels" spreadsheet table that decodes exactly
to the Square of Nine spiral formula; and a recovered 2001 working Excel
calculator (B12, `Gann Master Chart.xls`) implements the identical
spiral-from-center construction across dedicated price and time sheets, with
configurable conversion-factor and step parameters and explicit angle labels
(45.0°, 67.5°, etc.) tied to calendar dates — the clearest existing evidence
that the Square of Nine was operationalized by Gann's own tradition as both
a price tool and a genuine price-to-date time-conversion tool, not price-only.
Patrick Mikula's *Gann's Scientific Methods Unveiled: Volume 2* (B5)
describes a closely related **"Circle Chart"/"Square Chart"** construction —
the same spiral/hexagon geometry, built explicitly around planetary
longitudes rather than pure price arithmetic (see §6.3's astrology
discussion).

### 3.3 Gann angles

Also **absent from all ten public books**, also **directly disclosed by
Gann in A2.1**, in the same private-course chapter as the Square of Nine.
Named angles — 1×1 (45°), 2×1, 3×1, 4×1, and 8×1 (and by extension the full
symmetric family down to 1×8) — are defined as fixed rates of price gain or
loss per unit of time (Gann's own example: "the angle of 2×1 indicates a
gain of 6 points per year"). Crossing above or below the 1×1 angle is given
as the core strength/weakness signal: as long as price holds above its own
rising 1×1 angle drawn from a confirmed low, the trend is read as
structurally intact, and a close through it is the first warning the move
is over.

A secondary source (B10, a 2002 newsletter series by Jason Sidney, no
specific Gann book/course cited) adds a layer not found in the retrieved
portion of A2.1 itself: using Gann angles for **time projection, not just
price**. Each angle is mapped to a percentage of the swing's base time
interval — 1×1 = 100% of the base interval, 1×2 = 50%, 2×1 = 200%, 1×3 =
33.3%, 1×4 = 25%, 1×8 = 12.5% — with a worked numeric example (a 1991 Gulf
War low on the Australian SPI 200, price-doubling and angle-crossing dates
checked against a 1994 high). The same source cross-maps this onto the
retracement grid already described in §3.1 and explicitly labels the 3/8
and 5/8 fractions as equivalent to modern Fibonacci/Elliott framings, and
the 1/3 and 2/3 fractions as equivalent to Dow Theory — a useful bridge for
anyone comparing Gann's grid to more familiar modern technical-analysis
vocabulary.

### 3.4 Swing charts — the closest 1:1 match to anything Gann publicly taught

*45 Years in Wall Street* (1949, A9) discloses the **3-Day Chart** and
**9-Point Swing Chart** methods directly: plot only reversals of at least
three calendar days (with narrow exceptions near extremes), or, on a
separate chart, only reversals of at least nine points on the DJIA;
crossing the last 3-day or 9-point top/bottom is read as the trend signal.
This is a genuinely public-tier, fully disclosed technique, and it is the
single closest 1:1 match found in this entire research pass between
anything Gann personally, publicly taught and something already built in
GSPS's own codebase — `lib/gann/swingChart.ts`, whose own header comment
already cites "Gann's 3-day and 9-day swing charts" by name.

### 3.5 Volume and open-interest culmination rules

*How to Make Profits Trading in Commodities* (A8) gives four numbered rules
for spotting a top or bottom by volume of sales, plus a fifth rule on open
interest specifically: rising open interest into a low reads as
accumulation; falling open interest into a high on rising price reads as
distribution near a top. Two dated worked examples accompany these (a
February 1917 wheat/cotton break tied to the German U-boat declaration; a
July 1933 wheat break following a brokerage failure), plus a day-by-day
1938–1940 wheat volume table. This is the direct historical ancestor of
GSPS's `volumeClimax` criterion, though GSPS's specific implementation
(relative-volume-at-the-anchor-pivot) is a modern reconstruction of the
underlying idea, not a literal transcription of Gann's four rules.

## 4. Layer 3 — Timing: the withheld mechanism and its disclosed shell

Every source in this research pass agrees on one thing: **Gann never
publicly disclosed the actual mechanism behind his timing claims, in any
book, at any point across a forty-five-year career.** He states this
outright, in first person, in at least three separate books spanning 1909
to 1927, culminating in the all-capitalized refusal in *Tunnel Thru the
Air* quoted in §1. What follows here is not that mechanism — it is the
outer shell Gann was willing to disclose around it, plus what his private
course material (still only partially recovered — see Part II) adds.

### 4.1 The fixed annual calendar cycle

*Wall Street Stock Selector* (1930, A4) describes a fixed annual calendar
cycle of specific recurring dates — windows in early February, March, May,
June, August, September, November, and December — explicitly called "a
permanent cycle which does not change," to be watched for trend changes
every year regardless of what happened the year before. This is distinct
from anniversary-of-a-pivot timing (§4.2): it repeats on the same calendar
dates every year, independent of any individual stock's own history.

### 4.2 Anniversary dates

*45 Years in Wall Street* (A9) works this out year-by-year across
1929–1949: the month in which an extreme high or low occurred becomes a
date to watch on every subsequent year's calendar for that specific
instrument. *Tunnel Thru the Air* (A3) independently uses a stock's own
founding-date anniversary as a timing trigger in its fictional frame — a
narrower, company-specific version of the same underlying idea.

### 4.3 Duration-based "time square" thresholds

Also from A9: specific day-count bands (7–12, 18–21, 28–31, 42–49, 57–65,
85–92, 112–120, 150–157, 175–185 days) and month-count bands (1–5 years, or
15/22/34/42/48/49 months), checked against elapsed time from a high or low —
larger bands read as more significant when a move's duration lands inside
one. This is one of at least three separate, not-fully-reconciled
reconstructions of "the time factor" found across the whole corpus (see
also §4.4 and §6.2's Fourier reconstruction) — Gann himself never states a
single unifying formula tying these bands together.

### 4.4 Price/time squaring

The idea that a move is "square" when the number of elapsed bars matches
the size of the price move is stated as a governing principle repeatedly,
but — consistent with the withholding pattern — never given a single
disclosed formula for exactly *how* price and time units should be equated
(dollars to days, points to weeks, or something else). This is the specific
gap GSPS's own `lib/gann/timePriceSquare.ts` fills with a modern,
explicitly-labeled reconstruction (ATR-normalized price move compared
against elapsed bars) rather than a literal historical formula, because no
such formula survives in any recovered source.

### 4.5 What the still-unread private course material may contain

A2.1's table of contents lists a **"Master Time Factor"** chapter and a
**"Master Mathematical Price Time And Trend Calculator"** chapter in its
still-unread final ~360 of ~460 pages (only the first ~100 pages were
retrieved before an extraction limit cut the read off mid-chapter — see
Part II, §12). These are the most direct remaining lead toward whatever
mechanism Gann actually used, and closing this gap is named explicitly as
future work in both `GANN_HISTORICAL_SOURCES.md` and the implementation
blueprint proposed in Part III, §15.

## 5. Layer 4 — Confluence: the organizing principle behind everything else

No single layer above is ever presented, in any source, as sufficient on
its own. The organizing idea running through nearly the entire corpus is
that a price level becomes actionable when *multiple independent
coordinates agree* — a Square-of-9 resistance point, an old crossed level,
a retracement zone, and a trend-confirming angle, all converging near the
same price, is treated as a categorically stronger signal than any one of
those alone. *45 Years in Wall Street*'s worked 1929-top example (§3.1) —
where the actual historic top landed simultaneously on two independently
calculated percentage levels — is the clearest primary-source demonstration
of this principle in action: it is not a modern reconstruction layered onto
Gann's work; it is Gann himself demonstrating his own method's central
organizing idea on real, dated market data. This is also, not
coincidentally, the exact principle GSPS's `lib/signals/confluence/gann.ts`
module already implements structurally (see Part III, §13) — the one place
where GSPS's existing architecture is closest to Gann's own stated
philosophy, even though the module is currently display/audit-only rather
than something that gates a live trade.

## 6. Layer 5 — "Vibration": the least defined layer, and the one every secondary source disagrees about

Gann's 1909 headline claim was that every instrument has its own individual
"rate of vibration" — a claim he never once, in any recovered source,
reduces to a stated formula. This is the layer where the gap between "what
Gann said" and "what a formula would actually be" is widest, and where
later researchers, practitioners, and (per this report's own prior audit
pass) GSPS itself have filled the gap three separate, mutually
incompatible ways. Each is treated separately below, ranked by evidentiary
weight, because conflating them — treating "vibration" as one settled
concept rather than three competing guesses — is itself a source of
confusion in nearly every secondary work reviewed.

### 6.1 Digit-based numerology — weakest evidence

Reducing numbers to a single digit 1–9 by repeated digit-summing ("casting
out nines") and reading significance into the result. A forum fragment (B2)
demonstrates this applied to the 360° circle and to price-percentage
tables, but its central claimed discovery — "144 reduces to 9" — is true of
every multiple of 9 by simple construction, not an empirical finding about
markets. This is the weakest-evidenced of the three reconstructions. Its
one genuinely useful lead: B2 names L. Dow Balliett, a real, documented
early-1900s numerology author, as a plausible source of Gann's own
"vibration" vocabulary — a real historical thread worth noting even though
the technique itself carries little independent evidentiary weight.

Gann's own writing does supply real sacred-number content, distinct from
the digit-reduction technique above: *The Magic Word* (1950, A10) — a
devotional Bible concordance, not a market book — documents Gann's personal
framework of sacred numbers (3, 7 — explicitly "the sacred number", 9, 10,
12, 21) entirely outside any market context, including a hexagonal
geometric construction ("take seven pennies... one circle in the center and
six circles around it... draw another perfect circle around the rim")
presented as symbolizing the letters of the divine name, not as a price
chart — a real precursor or parallel to the hexagon/Square-of-9 geometry
that later shows up in his course material. A separate secondary source
(B8, a forwarded email quoting Gann's own "Master Egg Course," a paid
course distinct from A2.1's Master Stock Market Course) describes a
**time**, not price, technique built on the same numbers: counting days,
weeks, or months from swing extremes against degree-derived intervals
around a 360° circle (45°, 90°, 112.5°, 120°, 135°, 150°... 360°), and
subdividing a year by 2/3/4/8/16 with the 5th day/week/month/year singled
out as an "Ascension" point.

### 6.2 Fourier/spectral cycle analysis — the best-evidenced reconstruction

Directly named by Gann himself in *Tunnel Thru the Air* (A3) as "the cycle
theory, or harmonic analysis" — his stated operative technique. A modern
researcher's investigation (B1, Awodele 2013) demonstrates real
Fourier/spectral dominant-cycle decomposition on public-domain rainfall
data and on stock prices, and surfaces the strongest documentary
corroboration in the entire secondary-source set: a real 1922 newspaper
article and a 1926 Gann letter that name Henry Ludwell Moore, Arthur
Schuster, and Joseph Fourier by name as influences — historically real
figures in exactly the mathematical tradition this reconstruction claims to
represent. This is the single best-evidenced guess at what Gann's
"vibration"/harmonic-analysis claim actually corresponds to
mathematically — and, notably, it is a technique **nothing in GSPS
currently implements at all** (see Part III, §15.4).

### 6.3 Astrology — real, primary-sourced, but privately taught only

This is the layer that changed most substantially over the course of this
research pass, and it deserves full, direct treatment here rather than the
hedged "policy excludes it" framing this report's own predecessor document
used. **Real, primary evidence exists, in Gann's own voice, that he
personally used planetary-cycle timing as part of his private practice.**
It is absent from all ten of his public books — checked directly, including
both of his last two (1949's *45 Years in Wall Street* and 1954's *Economic
Forecaster*, the same year as the key private letter below) — but it is
directly present in material he sold or sent privately:

- **A2.1** (his private correspondence course): invokes Saturn's 30-year
  orbital cycle to explain market-cycle timing ("Saturn rules the products
  of the earth and causes extreme high or low prices") and, in a section
  titled "THE HUMAN BODY," equates the body's twelve openings to "the 12
  signs of the Zodiac." No planetary-longitude tables or aspect-based
  trading rules appear in the retrieved ~100-page portion — this is
  cosmological/numerological justification, not yet a worked ephemeris
  system. One sentence in this same document matters enormously for how
  to weight the whole topic: Gann himself writes, *"This Law is also backed
  with astrological proof, but anything that can be proved in any way or
  by any science is not correct unless it can be proved by numbers and by
  geometry"* — i.e., even in his own private teaching, to his own paying
  students, Gann subordinates astrology to number and geometry as the
  load-bearing layer. This is directly relevant to how any GSPS
  implementation should weight the two (see Part III, §15.5).

- **A2.3** (a signed, dated, NDA-bound March/November 1954 private client
  letter, headed "May Coffee Santos D") supplies the actual worked
  mechanism A2.1 only gestures at. This is the single strongest piece of
  astrological evidence in the entire corpus, and it is concrete enough to
  describe in full mechanical detail:

  - Several simultaneous **price-to-zodiac-degree conversion scales**,
    stated for the same traded price (8729): 1 point = 1°; 30 points = 1°;
    a named "Jupiter scale" of 12 points = 1°; 1 cent = 1°.
  - **Heliocentric and geocentric longitudes** for Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus,
    Neptune, Pluto, and Mars, each converted through those scales and
    checked against the price-derived degree for conjunction or
    opposition.
  - A **time-count technique** converting elapsed months into degrees via a
    stated points-per-month conversion rate (e.g., 50 points per month),
    tied explicitly to the 45° angle — a literal time/price-square
    mechanism with a disclosed conversion constant, directly answering part
    of the Layer-3 gap in §4.4.
  - Named **hard and soft aspects** (60°, 90°, 120°, 135°, 180°) between
    specific planet pairs, each keyed to a specific forecast calendar date.
  - **Mars "round trips"** — whole and quarter (90°) synodic cycles counted
    forward from a historical price low to flag anniversary reversal
    dates.
  - A **composite/averaging technique**: the mean of six heliocentric
    planetary longitudes, plus explicit half-sum midpoints between planet
    pairs.
  - An explicit statement withholding further material ("the CE AVERAGE,"
    "the MOF FORMULA") for higher-tier paying students, with a reminder of
    a signed non-disclosure agreement — the same withholding pattern
    documented publicly in A1 and A3, now shown operating *inside* paid
    private material too, not just toward the general public. This is a
    real, useful data point for Part III's licensing discussion: Gann
    himself treated tiers of his own private material as separately
    protectable, non-disclosure-bound intellectual property, decades before
    modern trade-secret practice formalized the same idea.

- **B5** (Patrick Mikula, *Gann's Scientific Methods Unveiled: Volume 2*):
  the most technically detailed secondary source on this topic, arguing —
  independently, before A2.3 was found in this pass — for essentially the
  same price-to-planetary-longitude conversion system (adding multiples of
  360° to a planet's zodiac longitude to get a price level), demonstrated
  against Gann's own 1948 soybean chart and a 1929 U.S. Steel passage. A2.3
  functionally confirms Mikula's central thesis with a primary source
  Mikula did not have access to.

- **B3** (the WD Gann Inc. company blog, from the entity holding Gann's
  actual copyrights and working papers): confirms, from the company's own
  description of Gann's real working papers, that his private planetary
  ephemeris was annotated with "planetary midpoints, declinations,
  Heliocentric transits, and the Lunar Nodes" — independent corroboration,
  from the party with the best claim to know, that this was a real,
  maintained working tool, not a one-off.

**The correct historical conclusion, resolved rather than left open**: the
public/private split held for Gann's entire forty-five-year career, start
to finish. It is not a late-career shift toward openness (as Mikula's book
originally framed it) — both of his last two books, 1949 and 1954 (the same
year as the A2.3 letter), are, on full direct reads, entirely silent on
astrology. He disclosed risk management and tape-reading freely, in print,
to the general public, for four and a half decades; he reserved the time
factor generally, and astrology specifically, for private client
correspondence and paid courses, consistently, the whole way through. This
is a real technique, personally used by Gann, with a worked primary-source
example now in hand — not a later researcher's invention retroactively
credited to him, and not a hypothesis GSPS would be inventing from nothing
if it chose to build it. What it has never been is public-facing material,
in Gann's own hands or since.

## 7. Layer 6 — Tape reading: the final arbiter

*Truth of the Stock Tape* (1923, A2) is explicit that beneath all of the
above, the tape itself — real order flow, volume, how a level actually
behaves when tested — is the final arbiter. Geometry and timing propose
candidate levels and candidate dates; the tape, and specifically volume
climax at a turning point (§3.5), confirms or denies them. This is the
layer GSPS's `volumeClimax` criterion is built to approximate, and it is
worth stating plainly that this ordering — geometry proposes, the tape
confirms — is itself a Gann-disclosed methodological principle, not just a
modern risk-management instinct layered on afterward.

## 8. General (non-Gann) cycle theory that legitimately informs the same territory

Because Gann himself named "cycle theory, or harmonic analysis" as his

- **Edward Dewey** (Foundation for the Study of Cycles), whose 1967
  manifesto (C6) sets out the load-bearing methodological standard this
  entire report treats as the right bar for validating *any* cyclical
  claim, Gann-derived or not: dominance, regularity of timing, repetition
  count, constancy of period, phase-resumption after distortion, wave-shape
  identity, cross-series clustering, and cycle synchrony. This is the
  strongest candidate in the entire non-Gann source set for actual reuse —
  it is a rigorous, independently sourced standard for distinguishing a
  real cycle from noise, directly applicable to validating any new
  time-based Gann criterion GSPS builds (see Part III, §15.4's proposed
  validation gate).
- **Ray Tomes** (Cycles Research Institute), whose "Towards a Unified
  Theory of Cycles" (C1) argues that non-linear response functions
  mathematically force driven systems toward integer-ratio harmonics, and
  who gives specific planetary-alignment periods (Jupiter-Venus-Earth ≈
  11.07 years; outer-planet realignment ≈ 171.4 years) as a candidate
  physical driver — independently, as a non-Gann author, arriving at the
  same "planetary alignment as market-cycle cause" reasoning Gann's own
  astrology material relies on. Useful as independent theoretical
  corroboration that the underlying idea is not unique to Gann or
  unfalsifiable on its face; Tomes' own out-of-sample track record (C2's
  2005 oil-price forecast, which missed) is honest evidence that this
  reasoning does not reliably produce accurate point forecasts even when
  applied rigorously and independently of Gann.
- Sources checked and explicitly **ruled out** as non-Gann or
  insufficiently substantive: B6 (general financial astrology, cites Gann
  only as inspiration), B7 (confirmed zero connection to Gann via

## 16. Where GSPS deviates from the historical method

Six concrete deviations, distinguishing genuine defects from defensible
modernizations:

1. **Astrology is entirely absent from the live product**, despite real,
   now-primary-sourced evidence (A2.1, A2.3, corroborated by B3 and B5)
   that Gann personally used it. This was previously framed in this
   codebase's documentation as an "explicit policy" exclusion; per the
   2026-09-15 direction confirmed for this report, that framing changes —
   the evidence base is now strong enough, and primary-sourced enough, to
   treat this as a genuine build candidate rather than an excluded topic
   (see §18.5).
2. **Square of 9 and Gann angles have been implemented without being
   labeled what they actually are**: private-course-tier material Gann
   never sold to the general public, not later researchers' reconstructions
   and not "the" public Gann method. The geometry itself is authentic — a
   2004 transcription of Gann's own course shows both in his own voice —
   but presenting either as *the* representative Gann technique, without
   noting its actual tier, inverts the real weight of evidence in his
   published writing, where old-level-crossing and resistance arithmetic
   dominate overwhelmingly instead.
3. **`EXECUTION_TIMEFRAME` is currently forced to `"1Hour"`** (see
   `AGENTS.md`'s temporary-overrides section), on a timeframe
   `docs/BACKTESTING.md` has directly measured to *invert* the scoring
   model's own verdict ranking. Every Gann criterion in the table above is
   currently being evaluated on the one timeframe already shown to mean
   the opposite of what it's supposed to. This is not a defect in any Gann
   technique — it is an unrelated, legitimate, temporary data-lag
   workaround with a side effect that currently undermines every criterion
   in this report equally.
4. **The current hand-set weight stopgap** (`DEFAULT_CRITERION_WEIGHTS`,
   also documented as a temporary override) pushes `gannAngleSlope`,
   `gannRetracementConfluence`, and `timePriceSquare` — three of the four
   most literally Gann-specific criteria — down to the weight floor, while
   `historicalSR` and `stopRoom` sit near the ceiling. This was a
   documented, reasoned stopgap off a single committed backtest run, not
   the proper in/out-of-sample process the codebase's own discipline
   requires — until that proper run exists, the live score is structurally
   weighted *against* Gann's specific geometric contributions relative to
   generic support/resistance and risk criteria, on thin evidence.
5. **The "time factor" is reconstructed two different, not-fully-
   reconciled ways** (`timeCycles.ts`'s fixed wheel counts,
   `timePriceSquare.ts`'s ATR-normalized squaring), neither of which is
   what either primary text actually discloses, since both explicitly
   withhold the real mechanism. The best-evidenced reconstruction —
   Fourier/spectral cycle analysis, the one technique Gann actually named
   directly — is the one GSPS does not implement at all.
6. **Money management is philosophically aligned but structurally
   different**: Gann's rules size risk in fixed dollar/point tiers by
   share-price bracket; GSPS uses a percentage-of-account, multi-ceiling
   model. This is a defensible, arguably necessary modernization for a
   multi-instrument, multi-account platform Gann never had to design for —
   named here for completeness, not flagged as a problem to fix.

## 17. The case for the numerology/astrology layer as licensed proprietary IP

The direction requested — build the numerology and astrology material into
the platform, keep it "far from public consumption," and license it as
GSPS's own intellectual property — is a coherent, defensible strategy, and
GSPS already has the architectural pattern this requires half-built.

**Why the evidence now supports this.** Section 6.3 above establishes that
this is not invented lore: Gann personally used a real price-to-planetary-
degree conversion system, evidenced by a signed 1954 client letter with
worked heliocentric/geocentric longitude calculations, corroborated
independently by a credentialed secondary researcher (Mikula) who reached
the same conclusion before that letter was found, and further corroborated
by the company holding Gann's actual archives describing his real,
annotated working ephemeris. Building this is not asserting a discredited
or fabricated technique — it is reconstructing a real historical system
from primary evidence, in exactly the same evidentiary posture GSPS already
treats `historicalSR` and `swingChartTrend`.

**Why "proprietary, not public" is the right framing, not a workaround.**
Gann's own practice (§6.3's A2.3 discussion) is direct precedent for this
exact structure: he explicitly withheld his most advanced material
("the CE AVERAGE," "the MOF FORMULA") for a higher paying tier, bound by a
signed NDA, while disclosing risk management and tape-reading freely in
print. A platform that builds a rigorous, internally-consistent Gann
astrology/numerology engine, keeps its internals undisclosed, and
surfaces only its *effects* (a refined signal, a tier-gated confluence
read) to end users is not distorting Gann's own approach to his most
advanced material — it is replicating its structure almost exactly.

**Why GSPS already has the mechanism this requires.** This is the most
important practical point: the redaction architecture already exists and
is already load-bearing for exactly this purpose. `lib/signals/
publicSummary.ts`'s `redactScanSignals` and `redactGannConfluence` already
strip a module's `evidence.inputs`/`explanationTrace` before any API
response leaves the server — the same mechanism already protects the
existing Digital Root/Vortex engine and the Gann Confluence layer's
internals today. `lib/constants/gspsTerminology.ts`'s `GSPS_TERM_REPLACEMENTS`
already maps internal-only vocabulary ("Digital Root," "Vortex") to generic
customer-facing copy ("GSPS Signal Calculation," "Signal Flow") for exactly
this reason. `docs/GANN_BLUEPRINT_TRACEABILITY.md`'s §17.1 audit already
identifies and names the tension between full signal transparency and
IP protection as a deliberate, existing design choice, not a gap — a
numerology/astrology engine is a new instance of a pattern this codebase
has already built and already reasoned about, not a new category of
problem.

**What "licensed as intellectual property" concretely means here**, and
what it does not: this is a product/brand strategy and a trade-secret
posture (keep the exact formulas, conversion constants, and ephemeris
methodology undisclosed and internal, the same way Gann kept his own),
not a claim of copyright over Gann's historical ideas themselves (which,
where drawn from his public-domain books, cannot be owned by anyone) or a
license of any third party's copyrighted secondary-source material (B5's
Mikula book, for instance, is actively copyrighted — GSPS's own
implementation must be built from the primary Gann sources and its own
original code, not transcribed from Mikula's book). The proprietary value
sits in GSPS's own specific implementation, calibration, and integration
of the underlying (largely public-domain or Gann's-own-uncopyrightable-
technique) ideas — the same posture already true of `digitalRoot.ts`'s
"GSPS hypothesis" framing today.

## 18. The recommended path forward: a from-scratch rebuild, not further patching

### 18.1 Why patch-in-place is the wrong instinct now

GSPS's Gann-alignment work to date has been built the way the project
owner describes it: in spurts, from partial "downloads" of Gann material
gathered over time, each pass adding or adjusting one or two criteria
against whatever source material was in hand at that moment. This
produced real, working value — six of nine live criteria trace directly to
disclosed Gann technique, as §15 shows — but it also produced the exact
symptoms visible in §16: criteria anchored inconsistently until a same-day
fix reached every caller (the `harmonicProximity` stale-anchor incident
`AGENTS.md` cites as the canonical cross-platform-consistency failure);
weights hand-tuned against a single run rather than derived from the
now-complete evidentiary picture; a threshold and weight stopgap adopted
under time pressure when the criteria set itself was still being
discovered; and — most importantly for this recommendation — every one of
these decisions made *before* the platform had access to the primary
material this report is built from. The 25-source research pass behind
this report is qualitatively different from everything that came before
it: it is the first time GSPS has had Gann's actual private-course
construction detail, a worked primary astrological example, and a
complete public-book survey in hand simultaneously, rather than reasoning
from fragments. Continuing to patch the existing nine-criterion structure
means permanently building on a foundation designed around an incomplete
picture. **The right move is to treat this report as the actual
specification and design the scoring/confluence layer from it directly,
rather than reconciling it after the fact against whatever the current
code happens to already do.**

This is a plan, not a mandate to touch `CRITERION_KEYS` this week —
every new or replacement criterion below must still clear the same
validation discipline already on file in `docs/
PROPOSAL_NEW_GANN_CRITERIA.md` (unmeasured → hypothesis → chronological
in/out-of-sample promotion) before it can affect a live verdict. What
changes is the *design*, not the discipline used to promote it.

### 18.2 Proposed architecture: the Gann Method Engine, v2

A single, complete rebuild of the Gann-derived layer, organized around
Part I's six layers rather than around the historical accident of which
criteria got built in which order. Each item below states what happens to
the existing module (kept as-is, refactored, replaced, or newly built) and
why.

**Layer 1 — Discipline.** *Kept, largely unchanged.* `lib/guided/
sizing.ts` and `lib/risk/*` are a legitimate, sound modernization of
Gann's own rules (§16, deviation 6) — no rebuild case exists here. Two
narrow additions, both cheap and both literal Gann rules with zero code
equivalent today:
- `lib/gann/ruleOfThree.ts` — the Rule of Three (§2.2): three consecutive
  closes against the prevailing trend as a reversal signal. Distinct from
  Sara's pattern taxonomy (see §19) — this counts closes across N bars of
  an established trend, not a single bar's shape.
- A "lost motion" refinement to `lib/trade/protocol-exit.ts`'s stop
  placement (§3.1): Gann's own stated reasoning (price overshoots a level
  by a bounded, typically small amount, hence a specific buffer) is
  directly testable against GSPS's existing stop-hit data before being
  adopted as a stop-sizing input.

**Layer 2 — Structure.** *Rebuilt around the refined hierarchy, Square of
9 re-tested, angles restored to real weight.*
- `lib/gann/retracement.ts` — extend to the 1949 refined percentage
  hierarchy (50% > 100% > 25% > 12.5% > 6.25% > 33⅓%/66⅔%, §3.1), not just
  the 1941 eighths/thirds grid it implements today. A ranked-importance
  hierarchy is a materially different scoring input than an unranked grid.
- `lib/gann/squareOf9.ts` — do not leave inert by default. Re-test as a
  fresh, role-aware, unweighted diagnostic (per the existing attribution
  pipeline), since the technique that failed under the pre-2026-09-10
  stale anchor has never been independently re-measured as its own
  criterion since that fix — the current inert status was inherited from
  a different criterion's (`harmonicProximity`'s) retirement, not from a
  clean test of Square of 9 on its own.
- `lib/gann/fans.ts` / `gannAngleSlope` — add the B10 time-projection layer
  (§3.3): a time-target field alongside the existing price-slope read,
  since nothing in GSPS today checks whether a price target implied by an
  angle is reached in the time the angle also implies.
- A new `lib/gann/ruleOfThreePoint.ts` (the "3-point rule," §3.1) — a
  stricter, threshold-based variant of `historicalSR`, not a replacement
  for it: a breakout must clear an old level by a fixed numeric buffer
  (or its ATR-normalized equivalent) before being trusted.
- A new `lib/gann/annualCycle.ts` (§4.1) — the fixed annual calendar cycle,
  the cheapest of the new candidates to test since it needs no pivot
  detection, only a date lookup.
- A new `lib/gann/anniversaryDates.ts` (§4.2) — a pivot's own month/day
  watched every subsequent year, cheap to test against existing
  pivot-detection code.

**Layer 3 — Timing.** *Add the missing best-evidenced reconstruction.*
- A new `lib/gann/spectralCycle.ts` — Fourier/dominant-cycle decomposition
  (§6.2), the reconstruction with the strongest historical citation
  (Moore/Schuster/Fourier) and the one glaring gap in GSPS's current time
  layer. This is genuinely new statistical code, not a criterion swap-in,
  and should be validated against Dewey's own methodological standard
  (§8's dominance/regularity/phase-resumption/synchrony checklist,
  `GANN_BLUEPRINT_BACKTEST_BIAS_AUDIT.md`'s existing statistical-rigor
  scope) before being treated as a candidate criterion at all.

**Layer 4 — Confluence.** *Kept structurally, extended in content.*
`lib/signals/confluence/gann.ts` already implements the right idea
(multi-coordinate agreement, display/audit-only by design) — it needs its
inputs extended (the new Layer-2/3 modules above feeding into it) rather
than its architecture changed.

**Layer 5 — "Vibration."** *The layer with the most real work to do,
and where the astrology/numerology-as-IP direction lives.*
- `lib/gann/digitalRoot.ts` — kept as GSPS's own explicit hypothesis, per
  its own existing framing. One concrete gap closed: persist a prior
  reading (blueprint's `digital_root_feature` table, already scoped as
  Milestone 3 in `GANN_BLUEPRINT_TRACEABILITY.md`, not yet built) so
  `classifyRootTransition`'s `VORTEX_FLOW_TRANSITION`/
  `ONE_RENEWAL_TRANSITION` types can ever actually fire — currently dead
  code paths.
- A new `lib/gann/astrology/` module family — the genuinely new,
  highest-leverage-for-differentiation piece of this whole rebuild:
  - `ephemeris.ts` — heliocentric and geocentric longitude calculation for
    the outer planets plus Mars (standard, publicly available orbital
    mechanics; the *proprietary* value is entirely in what GSPS does with
    the output, not the ephemeris math itself, which is not ownable).
  - `priceToDegreeConversion.ts` — the conversion-scale mechanism A2.3
    discloses (multiple simultaneous scales: 1 point = 1°, 30 points = 1°,
    a 12-points-per-degree "Jupiter scale," 1 cent = 1°) — GSPS's own
    calibration of which scale(s) apply to which instrument/price-range is
    the proprietary layer here.
  - `aspects.ts` — the named hard/soft aspect detection (60°/90°/120°/
    135°/180°) between calculated planetary and price-derived degrees.
  - `synodicCycles.ts` — Mars round-trip and other planet-pair synodic
    cycle counting from a historical anchor.
  - Every module in this family follows the *exact* redaction pattern
    already proven for Digital Root/Vortex (§17): confluence/context-only
    output, never an independent gate, `explanationTrace` computed
    server-side and stripped at the API boundary by the existing
    `publicSummary.ts` mechanism, internal vocabulary mapped through
    `GSPS_TERM_REPLACEMENTS` the same way "Digital Root" already is. This
    is a new instance of an existing pattern, not new IP-protection
    engineering.
  - Gann's own subordination of astrology to number/geometry (§6.3's
    quoted primary source: *"anything that can be proved... is not
    correct unless it can be proved by numbers and by geometry"*) should
    set this module's initial weight, if it clears validation at all: a
    confluence *tiebreaker/refiner*, ANDed alongside geometric criteria
    the way Digital Root already is inside `gannRetracementConfluence`
    (blueprint §7.4's safety rule) — never an independently sufficient
    signal, consistent with both Gann's own stated priority and GSPS's
    existing conservative-hypothesis-treatment precedent.

**Layer 6 — Tape reading.** *Kept unchanged.* `lib/gann/volumeClimax.ts`
is a sound reconstruction of a genuinely disclosed public technique;
no rebuild case exists here.

### 18.3 Resolving the two active temporary overrides as part of this rebuild, not separately

`AGENTS.md`'s two temporary overrides — the 1Hour execution-timeframe
forcing and the hand-set weight/threshold stopgap — were both adopted
under time pressure against an incomplete criteria set. Both should be
resolved *as part of* this rebuild rather than as separate, later cleanup:
the weight-rebalance stopgap in particular was fit to the *old* nine
criteria; a rebuilt criteria set needs its own fresh
`lib/backtest/propose-weights.ts` run against real chronological
in/out-of-sample data once enough rebuilt-criteria trades exist (n≥30
Execute bucket, the mandatory revert trigger already on file), not a
hand-carried-forward version of weights tuned to a scoring model this
report just recommended changing. The 1Hour override's own revert trigger
(real-time data purchase) is independent of this rebuild and should not be
conflated with it — but the rebuild should not be validated on the
currently-inverted 1Hour timeframe either; `docs/BACKTESTING.md`'s named
next step (a recent-window 1Hour run to separate a timeframe effect from a
regime effect) should happen before, not after, the new criteria are
committed to production weighting.

### 18.4 Sequencing

This is a multi-session, multi-phase build, not a single PR. Suggested
order, front-loading the cheapest, most independently-testable pieces:

1. Fixed annual calendar cycle and anniversary dates (§18.2, Layer 2) —
   cheapest to build and test, no new statistical machinery.
2. Rule of Three, 3-point rule, lost-motion stop refinement — literal,
   narrow, already have adjacent code to build from.
3. Refined percentage-resistance hierarchy — extends existing
   `retracement.ts` rather than adding a new module.
4. Square of 9 re-test (role-aware, fresh anchor) and Gann-angle
   time-projection layer.
5. Digital Root persistence (unlocks the two dead transition-type code
   paths).
6. Fourier/spectral cycle module — the largest net-new statistical
   investment, validated against Dewey's own rigor standard.
7. The astrology module family — the largest net-new build overall,
   sequenced last both because it is the most complex to get right and
   because by this point the platform will have a complete, freshly
   re-weighted geometric/timing layer to test any astrological confluence
   *against*, which is a more honest test than building it first in
   isolation.
8. A full `propose-weights.ts` re-run against the complete rebuilt
   criteria set, retiring the two temporary overrides in `AGENTS.md` as
   part of adopting its output.

Every step still goes through the existing unmeasured → hypothesis →
in/out-of-sample discipline before touching a live verdict — this
sequencing is about build order, not about a license to skip validation.

### 18.5 What changes about the astrology exclusion, explicitly

`docs/GANN_SARA_CONFLUENCE.md`'s "no new numerology without an authorized
specification" rule remains exactly correct and should not be weakened —
it exists to stop a future session from inventing Gann lore with no
evidentiary basis. What changes is that **this report itself is the
authorized specification** for the astrology/numerology material it
documents: every technique proposed in §18.2's Layer 5 traces to a
specific, cited primary source (A2.1, A2.3), not to an inference or a
secondary author's speculation. A future implementation session should
cite this report and its underlying `GANN_HISTORICAL_SOURCES.md` entries
the same way `digitalRoot.ts` already cites the GSPS Implementation
Blueprint — the precedent for "an authorized spec supersedes the default
no-new-numerology rule" already exists in this codebase's own history.

## 19. Sara Sniper Strat and the platform's other non-Gann indicators

### 19.1 What Sara Sniper Strat actually is, precisely

`lib/strat/patterns.ts` classifies every closed bar into exactly the
shape the project owner described wanting to keep: bars are typed **1**
(an inside bar — contained within the prior bar's range), **2U**/**2D**
(a directional bar — a higher high or a lower low, up or down), or **3**
(an outside bar — both a higher high and a lower low than the prior bar).
Patterns are then named by the sequence of these types across two or three
bars — `2-2` (a directional bar reversing on the next bar's opposite
extreme), `1-2-2` and `3-2-2` (the same reversal with an inside or outside
bar first, read as higher-conviction), `2-1-2` and `3-1-2` (continuation:
a directional or outside bar followed by an inside bar, breaking in the
original direction), and `PMG` (a distinct pivot-run pattern). This is, in
substance, the numeric bar-counting system publicly associated with "The
Strat" methodology (Rob Smith) — candlesticks read as a small alphabet of
1/2/3 shapes rather than named chart patterns — and it is a genuinely
different analytical primitive from anything in Part I's Gann material:
Gann's own disclosed techniques operate on swing highs/lows, elapsed time,
and price fractions of a range; Sara's system operates on the
containment/breakout relationship between one bar and its immediate
neighbor, entirely independent of where that bar sits relative to any
longer-term structure. **Per direct instruction, Sara Sniper Strat stays
in GSPS regardless of any overlap finding below** — the analysis that
follows is about where the two systems' *information content* overlaps
elsewhere in the scoring stack, not about whether to keep Sara, which is
settled.

### 19.2 Overlap analysis against Gann's own disclosed techniques

- **Rule of Three vs. Sara's continuation patterns**: related in
  mechanism (both count sequential bar relationships) but measuring
  different things. The Rule of Three counts consecutive *closes* against
  an established trend across an arbitrary number of bars, as a
  trend-exhaustion signal. Sara's `2-1-2`/`3-1-2` continuation patterns
  are a fixed two-to-three-bar containment shape, read as a
  trend-*continuation* trigger, not an exhaustion signal. These are
  complementary, not duplicative — implementing the Rule of Three (§18.2)
  adds no redundancy with `patternArmed`.
- **Sara's `2-2`/`1-2-2`/`3-2-2` reversal patterns vs. old-level crossing
  (`historicalSR`)**: genuinely different information. Old-level crossing
  is about *where* price is relative to a longer-term structural level;
  Sara's reversal patterns are about the *shape* of the last two or three
  bars, wherever price happens to be. A setup can have one without the
  other. No overlap to resolve.
- **`adxTrendStrength` vs. `swingChartTrend` vs. `gannAngleSlope`**: this
  is the one place in the *existing* nine-criterion set where real overlap
  exists, independent of Sara — all three are, at bottom, different ways
  of answering "is there a confirmed trend, and how strong is it." ADX/DMI
  is general technical analysis (explicitly built, per `AGENTS.md`'s
  cross-platform-consistency case study, to avoid leaning on PSAR/
  Supertrend alone in the separate Signal & Regime Engine); swing-chart
  trend is Gann's own literal 3-day/9-point method; Gann-angle slope is a
  geometric trend read. Per the cross-platform-consistency principle this
  report opened by citing (`AGENTS.md`), the right resolution is not to
  quietly retire two of the three as redundant without measurement — it is
  to let the rebuild's own attribution pipeline (§18.4) determine, with
  real data, whether all three carry independent information or whether
  one is a noisy proxy for another, and only then retire the redundant
  one, per the existing discipline that nothing gets removed from
  `CRITERION_KEYS` without a measured basis.
- **Digital Root/Vortex vs. the proposed astrology module**: both are
  reconstructions of the same underlying "vibration" claim (§6), and this
  is a real, foreseeable overlap risk the rebuild should design around
  from the start rather than discover after building both independently.
  The recommended approach (§18.2, Layer 5) treats astrology as a
  refinement layered *on top of* digital-root confluence where both are
  active, per Gann's own stated subordination of astrology to number and
  geometry (§6.3) — not two independent, competing "vibration" criteria
  scored side by side.

### 19.3 Recommendation

Keep Sara Sniper Strat exactly as it is — it is not a Gann technique, was
never claimed to be one, and creates no genuine redundancy with anything
in Part I's material once analyzed at the mechanism level rather than the
"they're both about candlesticks/bars" surface level. The one real overlap
worth resolving in the existing scoring stack (`adxTrendStrength` vs.
`swingChartTrend` vs. `gannAngleSlope`) should be resolved the same way
this report recommends resolving everything else in the rebuild: with
fresh attribution data under the new architecture, not by assumption. The
one overlap risk worth designing around proactively, before it is built
rather than after (digital root vs. astrology), has a concrete resolution
already specified in §18.2.
